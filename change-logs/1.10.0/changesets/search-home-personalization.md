## zh-CN

搜索首页新增个性化内容：按账号记录最近搜索（最多 15 条），并根据近 90 天的使用频次推荐常用频道、私信和联系人（7 天半衰期衰减，最多 10 条）。最近搜索支持逐条编辑和一键清空，清空不影响常用推荐；已归档、未加入、不可读或已隐藏的对象不会出现在推荐中。

## en

Search Home is now personalized: recent searches are kept per account (up to 15), and frequently used channels, DMs, and contacts from the last 90 days are recommended (7-day half-life decay, up to 10). Recent searches can be edited individually or cleared all at once without affecting recommendations; archived, unjoined, unreadable, or hidden targets never appear.
