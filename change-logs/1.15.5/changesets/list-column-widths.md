## zh-CN

平板和大屏上，列表栏宽度改成和网页一致：频道列表默认 240，动态和搜索列表默认 380，并且各自记住拖动后的宽度；搜索在选中结果前占满整个宽度。

## en

On tablets and large screens, list columns now match the web's widths. The channel list defaults to 240 and the Activity and Search lists to 380, and each remembers its own dragged width. Search fills the whole width until you pick a result.
