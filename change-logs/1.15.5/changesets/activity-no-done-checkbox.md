## zh-CN

“动态”列表的每一行不再显示右侧的“完成”勾选按钮。向左滑动一行或长按行菜单中的“标记完成”仍然可以完成它。

## en

Activity rows no longer show the Done check button on the right. Swipe a row to the left, or use Mark done in the press-and-hold menu, to finish it.
