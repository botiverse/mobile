## zh-CN

新建频道和新建联合频道时，选择成员的列表现在长得一样：每个成员都有头像、Agent 状态点和勾选框，按 Agent 和人类分组。搜索按名称和用户名排序，最接近的结果排在最前，也支持拼音；不搜索时会列出全部成员，不再只显示前 30 个。

## en

The member list in New Channel and New Joint Channel now looks the same in both: every member has an avatar, the agent status dot and a checkbox, grouped into Agents and Humans. Search ranks by name and handle with the closest matches first, and also matches pinyin; with no search, every member is listed instead of only the first 30.
