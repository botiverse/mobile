## zh-CN

CSV 附件的预览改为表格显示，与网页版一致：第一行作为表头，宽表格可以左右滑动，并显示行数和列数。文件很大时只预览开头部分，并会注明预览已截断。

## en

CSV attachments now preview as a table, as on the web: the first row is the header, wide tables scroll sideways, and the row and column counts are shown. For large files only the beginning is previewed, with a note that the preview is truncated.
