## zh-CN

动态的"未读"里，这次进入后看过的会话会留在原位并显示为已读，不会马上消失；"全部标为已读"后列表也保持不变。切换筛选、离开动态、下拉刷新或切换服务器后再重新整理。

## en

In Activity's Unread filter, conversations you read during this visit now stay in place, shown as read, instead of disappearing right away, and "Mark all read" keeps the list as it is. The list is tidied up when you switch filters, leave Activity, pull to refresh or switch servers.
