## zh-CN

从动态点进频道、私信、提及或子区时，动态里看到的那条消息会立刻显示，不再先出现空白页或"暂无消息"；完整内容加载好后自动替换。确实没有任何已知内容时，会显示加载中，而不是空白。

## en

Opening a channel, DM, mention or thread from Activity now shows the message you saw in Activity right away, instead of a blank page or "No messages yet"; the full conversation replaces it once it loads. When nothing is known yet, the page shows a loading indicator rather than staying blank.
