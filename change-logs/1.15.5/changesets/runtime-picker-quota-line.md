## zh-CN

创建 Agent 和更换运行时时，选择计算机或运行时的列表会在每个选项下显示额度，例如"Claude Max · 5 小时 剩 72% · 本周 剩 40%"；已用完的窗口会显示何时恢复。选项不超过 5 个时直接列出，6 到 10 个时从底部弹出列表，更多时可以搜索。

## en

When you create an agent or change its runtime, each computer or runtime in the picker now shows its remaining quota, such as "Claude Max · 5h 72% left · Week 40% left", and a used-up window says when it resets. Up to 5 options are listed in place, 6 to 10 open a list from the bottom of the screen, and more can be searched.
