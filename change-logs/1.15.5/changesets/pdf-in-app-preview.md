## zh-CN

PDF 可以直接在应用内预览。点对话中的 PDF 文件卡片，或频道「文件」面板中的 PDF，会打开 PDF 预览：先下载文件并显示进度，然后按页纵向滚动显示，支持双指缩放和拖动，底部显示当前页码（例如「3 / 12」）。右上角菜单仍可下载、分享或在其他应用中打开。受密码保护或已损坏的 PDF 会显示说明，并提供「在其他应用中打开」按钮。

## en

PDFs now open in an in-app preview. Tapping a PDF card in a conversation, or a PDF in a channel's Files panel, opens the PDF preview: the file downloads with progress, then its pages show in a vertical scroll with pinch-to-zoom and pan, and the current page shows at the bottom (for example "3 / 12"). The menu still offers Download, Share and opening the file in another app. A password-protected or damaged PDF shows a clear message with an "Open in another app" button.
