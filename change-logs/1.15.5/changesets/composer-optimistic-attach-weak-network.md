## zh-CN

弱网下选图后，图片会立即出现在输入框上方并显示上传进度，不再等待服务器上传限制请求返回；上传限制会按服务器缓存并在打开输入框时预取。

## en

On a weak network, a picked image now appears above the composer immediately with upload progress, instead of waiting for the server upload-limits request. Limits are cached per server and prefetched when the composer opens.
