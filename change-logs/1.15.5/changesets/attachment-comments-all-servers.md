## zh-CN

附件评论现在对所有服务器开放，不再只限于个别服务器，与网页端一致。评论入口和网页端保持相同：Markdown、文本和代码、CSV、XLSX、HTML 附件可以评论；图片、PDF、音频和无法预览的文件与网页端一样不提供评论。是否允许评论仍由服务器的权限决定。

## en

Attachment comments are now available on every server instead of a single one, matching the web. The entry points follow the web: Markdown, text and code, CSV, XLSX and HTML attachments can be commented on; images, PDFs, audio and files without a preview have no comments, as on the web. The server still decides who may comment.
