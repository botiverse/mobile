## zh-CN

移除“点击动态跳转到下一条未读”的行为。再次点按“动态”标签页或点按其标题时，与其他标签页一样，列表回到顶部，不再逐条跳到未读行并高亮。

## en

Removed jump-to-next-unread from Activity. Tapping the Activity tab again, or its title, now scrolls the list to the top like the other tabs, instead of stepping through unread rows and highlighting them.
