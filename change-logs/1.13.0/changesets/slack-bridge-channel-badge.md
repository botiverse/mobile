## zh-CN

已接入 Slack 桥接的频道现在会在频道列表的频道名后、以及频道聊天页标题旁显示「SLACK」标签，与网页端侧边栏一致。标签只在服务端返回该频道的 Slack 桥接信息时出现，普通频道、私信与话题回复不受影响。

## en

Channels bridged to Slack now show a "SLACK" tag after the channel name in the channel list and next to the channel chat header title, matching the Web sidebar. The tag only appears when the server reports a Slack bridge for that channel; regular channels, DMs and reply threads are unaffected.
