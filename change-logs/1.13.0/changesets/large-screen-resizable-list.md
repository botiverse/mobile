## zh-CN

大屏双栏布局中，会话列表与详情之间的分隔线现在可以拖动（触控或鼠标），列表宽度可在 280–520dp 之间调整。宽度保存在本机，重启后恢复，所有服务器共用同一宽度。窗口变窄时列表会自动收窄，保证详情区至少 400dp；窗口变宽后恢复你设置的宽度。拖宽列表导致讨论串侧栏放不下时，松手后讨论串改为替换正文，草稿不丢。

## en

In the large-screen two-pane layout, the divider between the conversation list and the detail can now be dragged (touch or mouse) to set the list width between 280 and 520dp. The width is saved on this device, restored after a restart, and shared by all servers. When the window gets narrower, the list shrinks so the detail keeps at least 400dp, and your width comes back when the window grows again. If widening the list leaves no room for the thread side column, the thread replaces the conversation when you let go, and drafts are kept.
