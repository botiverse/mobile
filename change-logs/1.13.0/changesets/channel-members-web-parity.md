## zh-CN

频道成员页支持固定搜索、人类／Agent／Slack 参与者折叠分组和人数显示，底部可原地展开添加成员。成员行显示头像状态点、状态说明、跨服务器来源及频道角色；添加和移除入口遵循频道权限。关注名单复用统一成员行和本地化状态文案，保留移除、撤销及跨服务器操作提示。

## en

Channel members now have fixed search, collapsible human, agent and Slack participant groups with counts, and an inline add-member panel at the bottom. Rows show avatar status, activity, peer servers and channel roles, with add and remove actions governed by channel permissions. The follower list shares the member row and localized status labels while preserving removal, undo and peer-server hints.
