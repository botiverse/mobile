## zh-CN

搜索范围过大时，可一键改为按最新排序重新搜索，保留查询内容及频道、发送者、范围和日期筛选。已按最新排序时不会重复显示该操作。

## en

When a search is too broad, switch to newest-first search with one action while keeping the query, channel, senders, scope, and date filters. The action is hidden when results are already sorted by newest.
