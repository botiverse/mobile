# 中文

联合频道在超出成员上限时，频道内顶部显示提示横幅：宽限期内显示截止时间与恢复方式（任一服务器管理员升级或一个免费服务器退出），宽限期结束后频道变为只读。

# English

Joint channels over their member limit now show an in-channel banner: during the grace period it states the deadline and the fix (an admin upgrades or a free server leaves); after the deadline the channel is read-only.
