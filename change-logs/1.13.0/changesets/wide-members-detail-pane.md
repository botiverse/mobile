## zh-CN

大屏桌面模式下，点击成员会在名单旁显示详情，保留搜索、分组折叠与滚动位置。切换成员显示对应资料，返回关闭详情；窄屏新打开成员仍使用原有整页导航。

## en

In large-screen desktop mode, member details open beside the roster while preserving search, collapsed groups, and scroll position. Selecting another member switches profiles, and Back closes the detail pane. Opening members on compact screens retains full-page navigation.
