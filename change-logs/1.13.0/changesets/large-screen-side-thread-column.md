## zh-CN

大屏桌面模式下，空间足够时（窗口宽至少 1200dp，且正文区扣除列表后仍放得下），从频道或私聊打开讨论串会显示在正文右侧的独立一栏，可以边看频道边回复；返回或讨论串的关闭按钮只关这一栏。窗口变窄时讨论串改为替换正文，返回可回到频道。

## en

On large screens in desktop mode, when there is enough room (a window at least 1200dp wide and space left beside the list), opening a thread from a channel or DM now shows it in its own column next to the conversation, so you can read the channel while replying. Back or the thread's close button closes only that column. When the window gets narrower, the thread replaces the conversation instead, and Back returns to the channel.
