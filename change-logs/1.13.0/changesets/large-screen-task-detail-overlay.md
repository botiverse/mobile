## zh-CN

大屏桌面模式下，从任务看板打开任务时，任务讨论会在看板上方的面板中显示，看板的筛选、滚动位置和选中项都会保留；点空白处、关闭按钮或返回即可回到看板。之前每次打开任务都会跳到整页。

## en

On large screens in desktop mode, opening a task from the Tasks board now shows its thread in a panel above the board, keeping the board's filters, scroll position, and selection. Tap outside, the close button, or Back to return. Before, every task opened as a full page.
