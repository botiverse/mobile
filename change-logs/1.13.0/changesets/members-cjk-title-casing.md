## zh-CN

中文和日文界面中，成员页的标题和选项不再把夹带的英文强制转成大写（例如“重启 Agent”不再显示成“重启 AGENT”）；重置会话的说明改用不带括号的写法，换行后不再出现突兀的留白。

## en

In Chinese and Japanese, Members page titles and options no longer force embedded English words into all caps (for example, "重启 Agent" no longer shows as "重启 AGENT"). The reset-session description is reworded without parentheses so it wraps without odd gaps.
