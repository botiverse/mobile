## zh-CN

底部最后一个 tab 换成当前服务器，点一下或长按就能打开服务器面板切换服务器，切换后停留在当前 tab；成员、Computers、设置也在面板里，打开后可以返回到原来的页面。

## en

The last tab at the bottom is now your current server. Tap or long-press it to open the server panel and switch servers; you stay on the tab you were on. Members, Computers and Settings are in the panel too, and Back returns you to where you were.
