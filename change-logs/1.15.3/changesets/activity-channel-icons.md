## zh-CN

动态里的频道显示自己的图标（联合频道、私有频道）：联合频道显示分支图标，私有频道显示锁，公开主页可读的频道显示带地球的 #，其他频道仍是 #。频道里的话题保留角上的话题标记，私信不变。

## en

Channels in Activity show their own icon (joint and private channels): a joint channel shows the branch icon, a private channel the lock, a channel readable on the public page the # with a globe, and other channels keep the #. Threads in a channel keep their corner thread mark; DMs are unchanged.
