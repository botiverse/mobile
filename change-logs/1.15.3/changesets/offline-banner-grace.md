## zh-CN

网络短暂抖动（如 Wi-Fi 与蜂窝网络切换）时不再闪一下「无网络连接」提示；断网持续 3 秒后才显示，恢复联网后立即消失。

## en

A brief network blip, such as switching between Wi-Fi and cellular, no longer flashes the "No internet connection" banner. It now appears only after 3 seconds offline and disappears as soon as the connection is back.
