## zh-CN

回复别人新开的私聊后（包括在它的 thread 里回复），这个私聊会马上出现在私信列表里，不用再等对方发下一条消息。

## en

After you reply in a direct message someone else started (including in one of its threads), it shows in your Direct Messages list right away, instead of only after they write again.
