## zh-CN

计算机名、Agent 名、模型名等信息长按即可复制：在 Agent、成员和计算机详情页，长按名称、@用户名、计算机、模型、运行时、提供方、命令等值会复制原始内容并提示「已复制」；可点击的值（如计算机名）轻点仍会打开。

## en

Long-press to copy names and other details: on agent, member and computer pages, long-pressing a name, @handle, computer, model, runtime, provider, command or similar value copies it and shows "Copied". Values you can tap, such as a computer name, still open on a tap.
