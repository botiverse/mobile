## zh-CN

点“下一个未读 thread”跳到一条很长的消息时，现在会停在它的回复区（“N 条回复”和回复预览）附近，不再只显示消息开头。

## en

Tapping the next-unread-thread pill on a long message now lands on its replies block (the reply count and previews) instead of the top of the message.
