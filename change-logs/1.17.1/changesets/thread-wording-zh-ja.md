## zh-CN

中文界面里的“thread”统一叫「消息列」，和网页版一致：例如「下一个未读消息列」「最早的未读消息列」「搜索此消息列…」，不再出现英文 thread、“回复串”或“讨论串”。

## en

The Chinese interface now calls a thread 消息列 everywhere, as the web does ("Next Unread Thread", "Search this thread…" and others no longer mix in the English word or other terms). The Japanese thread search placeholder and one settings line now use the web's wording too.

## ja
