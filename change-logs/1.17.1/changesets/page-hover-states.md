## zh-CN

用鼠标或触控板时，把指针移到一条消息、「动态」里的一行或侧栏的服务器图标上，它会显示浅色的悬停底色；消息里的链接在指针下会变深，下划线也变成完整的颜色。手指触摸不会留下悬停效果。

## en

With a mouse or trackpad, a message, a row in Activity and the server icon in the side rail now show a light hover fill under the pointer, and a link in a message turns deeper with a full-colour underline. Touching with a finger never leaves a hover look behind.
