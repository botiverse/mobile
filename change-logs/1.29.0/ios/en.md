✨ **New**
- Unified web panel: tapping a third-party link or a Raft App in a conversation opens a bottom-rising web panel with a dimmed one-line source strip at the top—drag up for full screen, or drag down or tap the strip to return while keeping the page open (marked "Page still open" under the message). Third-party sites and Raft Apps use isolated storage partitions.
- "Share to Raft" Share Extension & share sheet suggestions: share text, links, images, videos, and files (including `.zip` archives) from apps such as WeChat, Photos, Files, and Safari straight into any Raft channel, DM, thread, or task; recent Raft channels and DMs also appear as suggestions at the top of the share sheet and clear automatically on sign-out or account switch.
- Share from any file preview & Open from download toasts: the overflow menu in image, video, document, and general file previews now includes Share (downloading uncached files first), and download completion toasts include an Open button that works even after you leave the page.
- Agent Apps tab one-row view & Revoke access: each App now takes one row summarizing what it can do; server owners, admins, and the agent's creator can revoke all grants at once with confirmation or revoke individual grants under Details.
- Agent migration log & interactive chart: server owners and admins can open "View migration log" from an agent profile or a Computer's page to inspect moves on a zoomable chart or filtered list, and dismiss failed migration notices with ×.
- Offline Computer recovery card on agent profiles: when an agent's Computer is offline, the agent's profile now shows the web's recovery card naming the offline Computer, when it went offline, and two recovery steps with a copyable restart command.
- Native "Send to an agent" sheet for announcements: tapping a send-prompt link in an announcement opens a native sheet that suggests a default agent, previews recent messages, lets you edit the prompt, and sends directly to that DM.
- Tappable Lounge references: `lounge:@agent` references in messages—and references to threads, tasks, or messages inside that Lounge—open straight to that place.
- iPad side-pane memory: on iPad, each conversation remembers on this device which reply thread, Artifact (including a pinned version), or profile (and agent tab) was open beside it and restores it when you switch back.

🔧 **Improved**
- Artifacts home & viewer: redesigned to match the web with compact 16:10 grid tiles and thumbnail list rows, a collapsible "For you" section and date groups, per-server remembered Conversation / Author / Status / "Relevant to me" filters, and settle/unsettle from the card menu; pinch-to-zoom now works in the Artifact viewer without turning on Desktop Mode, redundant "Open in browser" entries are removed, and Artifact fallback pages open authenticated in-app.
- Tasks list & conversation header: the Tasks list now uses compact web-style rows so more tasks fit on an iPhone screen; short task conversations start at the top with Status and Assignee on one row, avatar plus name ("You" for yourself), "Not recorded" when no source was recorded, and "Beginning of replies".
- Settings, Appearance & Account: Settings › Appearance is consolidated into a single cleaner section and adds an account-synced Keyboard section ("Send messages with" Enter or ⌘Enter / Ctrl+Enter) when running on Mac or with a hardware keyboard connected; Settings › Account places Change/Set password below the profile card, gives Connected accounts its own section, and moves Product analytics above Session.
- iPad, wide screens & landscape room: iPhone and iPad share one wide-layout breakpoint, with phone-sized text for touch on iPad; wide headers fold into a single baseline with aligned split-view top dividers, and the composer no longer stacks extra resting padding above the bottom safe area, giving more reading room on compact landscape screens; every side pane ends with a Close (×) button, collapsed sidebars leave an "Expand sidebar" strip, and searchable pickers open right under their button on wide windows.
- Smoother navigation bar transitions: entering conversations, members, channel details, Activity, and Tasks pre-fills the native navigation bar title, icon, and buttons before the push animation starts so empty bars no longer flash in.
- Activity, GIFs, Mermaid & menus: iPhone Activity rows use one-line previews, while touch iPads no longer keep a permanent Mark as Done button on every row; newly sent GIFs animate inline and pause off-screen; Mermaid diagrams use the message font with node text wrapping and richer formatting; added the 🍻 reaction sticker with dark-mode-readable outlines on all eight stickers; aligned menu order, header buttons, and English sentence-case labels with the web; sending in a conversation marked unread clears the unread mark.
- Faster & lighter: chat images resolve URLs in batches and reuse disk/memory cache across full-screen previews, restart paints real avatars and server icons on the first frame, offline conversations page bounded local message windows with gap rows, Home scrolls more smoothly after resume, high-res photos decode faster when opening preview or edit mode, silent catch-up runs on reconnect while skipping redundant foreground refetches within 60 seconds when connected, and pinned content refreshes on events instead of polling every minute.
- Agents, Computers & external channels: runtime pickers read each Computer's live reported capabilities; streamlined Add Computer opens directly on the connect command; web-aligned agent profile facts, hover cards, and stable form spacing; channel Members adds a direct "Invite people outside" button, Connect your servers checks Free-plan slots, refusal errors are localized, and "external channel" and Lounge copy match the web everywhere.
- Everyday polish: unpreviewable files replace "Open in browser" with "Open in another app"; hardware keyboard `@`/`#` suggestions support arrow keys, Enter/Tab, and Esc; search results and message hover toolbars show mouse hover states; attachment downloads share the app download centre and empty states match the design system.
