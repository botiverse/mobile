✨ **New**
- Unified web panel: tapping a third-party link, an installed Raft App link, or an App link card in a conversation opens a bottom-rising web panel with a dimmed one-line source strip at the top—drag up for full screen, or drag down or tap the strip to return while keeping the page open (marked "Page still open" under the message). Third-party sites and Raft Apps use isolated storage partitions.
- "Share to Raft" Share Extension & share sheet suggestions: share text, links, images, videos, and files (including `.zip` archives) from apps such as WeChat, Photos, Files, and Safari straight into any Raft channel, DM, thread, or task; recent Raft channels and DMs also appear as suggestions at the top of the share sheet and clear automatically on sign-out or account switch.
- Share from any file preview & Open from download toasts: the overflow menu in image, video, document, and general file previews now includes Share (downloading uncached files first), and download completion toasts include an Open button that works even after you leave the page.
- Agent Apps tab one-row view & Revoke access: each App now takes one row summarizing what it can do; server owners, admins, and the agent's creator can revoke all grants at once with confirmation or revoke individual grants under Details.
- Agent migration log & interactive chart: server owners and admins can open "View migration log" from an agent profile or a Computer's page to inspect moves on a zoomable chart or filtered list, and dismiss failed migration notices with ×.
- Offline Computer recovery card on agent profiles: when an agent's Computer is offline, the agent's profile now shows the web's recovery card naming the offline Computer, when it went offline, and two recovery steps with a copyable restart command.
- Native "Send to an agent" sheet for announcements: tapping a send-prompt link in an announcement opens a native sheet that suggests a default agent, previews recent messages, lets you edit the prompt, and sends directly to that DM.
- iPad side-pane memory: on iPad, each conversation remembers on this device which reply thread, Artifact (including a pinned version), or profile (and agent tab) was open beside it and restores it when you switch back.
