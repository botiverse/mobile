## zh-CN

按下或鼠标悬停时，颜色立即变化，不再渐变。列表里不可选的成员或服务器，头像会和文字一起变淡。多选消息时，工具栏的全选框和左边缘的距离与右侧关闭按钮一致。

## en

Pressing or hovering with a mouse changes the color at once, with no fade. When a person or server in a list can't be chosen, its avatar dims along with its text. When you select messages, the toolbar's select-all box sits as far from its edge as the close button does at the other end.
