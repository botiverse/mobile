## zh-CN

在平板上，Artifact 在会话旁边打开时，页面加载完成后，它的顶栏与旁边会话的顶栏底边对齐，不再比会话的顶栏低一截。用触控和用鼠标或触控板时都对齐。需要 Raft 网页端同步更新后生效，在此之前仍是原来的样子。

## en

On tablets, when an Artifact is open beside a conversation, its top bar now lines up with the conversation's top bar once the page has loaded, instead of ending lower. It lines up both when you use touch and when you use a mouse or trackpad. This takes effect once the matching Raft web update is live; until then it looks as before.

## ja
