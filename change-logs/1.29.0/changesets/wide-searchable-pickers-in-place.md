## zh-CN

在平板、电脑等宽屏窗口上，可以搜索的选择列表改为在你点按的字段或按钮下方直接展开，和网页版一致，不再从屏幕底部弹出面板。包括：创建 Agent 和运行时设置里的模型、计算机等选择，操作卡片表单里的选择，任务的频道、创建者、负责人筛选和任务卡片上的负责人选择，任务顶部的负责人，Artifacts 首页按对话和作者的筛选，以及 Agent 迁移记录的筛选。手机上仍从底部弹出。

## en

On wide windows such as tablets and computers, searchable pickers now open right under the field or button you tapped, as on the web, instead of as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. This covers the model, computer and similar pickers in Create agent and runtime settings, choices in action card forms, the Tasks channel, creator and assignee filters and the assignee picker on task cards, the assignee in a task's header, the Artifacts filters by conversation and author, and the agent migration log filters. Phones still open them from the bottom.

## ja
