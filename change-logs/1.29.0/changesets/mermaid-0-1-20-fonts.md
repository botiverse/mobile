## zh-CN

消息里的 Mermaid 图按正文字体排版：方框正好装下文字，粗体前不再空一大截。子图标题里的换行和粗体能显示，子图连到自己的线也画出来了；带主题、间距等配置的图不再退回成代码块。

## en

Mermaid diagrams in messages are laid out with the message font: boxes fit their text, and bold words no longer follow a wide gap. Subgraph titles show their line breaks and bold text, a subgraph's link to itself is drawn, and diagrams with a theme, spacing or other settings no longer fall back to code.

## ja
