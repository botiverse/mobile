## zh-CN
- 新增 iOS 系统「分享到 Raft」扩展：支持从微信、相册、文件、Safari 等应用把文字、链接、图片、视频及任意文件（含 `.zip` 压缩包）直接分享进 Raft，选择目标会话（频道、私信、讨论串或任务，支持私有部署）后发送。

## en
- Added the iOS "Share to Raft" system Share Extension, allowing you to share text, links, images, videos, and arbitrary files (including `.zip` archives) from apps like WeChat, Photos, Files, and Safari directly into any Raft conversation (including private deployments).
