## zh-CN

Mac 电脑版在打开网页内容（例如右侧的 Artifact 或设置里的网页页面）时，用中文拼音等输入法打字或调整窗口大小，不会再卡死。

## en

The Mac app no longer freezes when you type with an input method such as Chinese Pinyin, or resize the window, while web content is open (for example an Artifact on the right or a web page in Settings).
