## en

On tablets, unfolded foldables and phones held sideways, page headers are now shorter when you use touch (56 → 48), so more of the content fits on screen, and the buttons and icons in them are slightly smaller to match. The workspace avatar at the top left now lines up with the sidebar icons below it.
