## zh-CN

在平板、展开的折叠屏和横屏手机上用手指操作时，页面顶部的标题栏更矮了（56 → 48），能看到更多内容；标题栏里的按钮、图标、私聊和成员页的头像、设置页的图标块也随之收小，与频道图标一样大。顶部栏左上角的工作区头像与下方侧边栏的图标对齐。

## en

On tablets, unfolded foldables and phones held sideways, page headers are now shorter when you use touch (56 → 48), so more of the content fits on screen, and the buttons, icons, the avatar in direct messages and member pages, and the icon tile on settings pages are smaller to match, the same size as the channel icon. The workspace avatar at the top left now lines up with the sidebar icons below it.

## ja
