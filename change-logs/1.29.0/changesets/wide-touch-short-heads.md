## zh-CN

在平板、展开的折叠屏和横屏手机上用手指操作时，页面顶部的标题栏更矮了（56 → 48），能看到更多内容；按钮大小不变。

## en

On tablets, unfolded foldables and phones held sideways, page headers are now shorter when you use touch (56 → 48), so more of the content fits on screen. Buttons keep their size.
