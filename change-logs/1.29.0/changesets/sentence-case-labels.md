## zh-CN

英文界面的按钮、菜单和标题改为与网页版一致的句首大写写法，例如 Reply in thread、Load more、Copy link、As task、New task、In progress。Raft、Pro、Artifacts 等名称仍保持大写。其他语言不受影响。

## en

English buttons, menus and titles now use sentence case as on the web, such as Reply in thread, Load more, Copy link, As task, New task and In progress. Names such as Raft, Pro and Artifacts keep their capitals. Other languages are unchanged.
