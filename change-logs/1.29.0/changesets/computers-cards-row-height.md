## zh-CN

在宽窗口的电脑页卡片视图中，同一行的卡片现在一样高，和网页一致，「升级到 v…」按钮固定在卡片底部。版本落后的电脑，信息行不再把系统名截成两个字母又把「有可用更新」拆成两行：放不下时「有可用更新」完整地换到下一行，系统名照常显示。

## en

On the Computers page's cards in a wide window, the cards in a row are now the same height, as on the web, with "Upgrade to v…" at the bottom of its card. An outdated computer's facts line no longer cuts the OS name to two letters while splitting "Update available" across two lines: when it doesn't fit, "Update available" moves whole to the next line and the OS name stays readable.

## ja
