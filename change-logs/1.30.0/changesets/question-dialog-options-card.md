## zh-CN

问题卡弹窗的选项改为和网页一样放在圆角卡片里：校验出错时卡片边框变红，选项超过 5 个才出现搜索框，回到上一题再点同一个答案也会直接进入下一题；题目说明显示在题目下方、选项上方，出错时也不会被提示盖掉。平板上提交后，底部左侧显示「已提交 · 撤销」。

## en

Question cards' answer dialog now shows the options in a rounded card, as on the web: the card's border turns red on a validation error, a search box appears only with more than 5 options, and tapping the same answer again after going back moves on to the next question. A question's description sits under the question, above the options, and stays visible next to an error. On a tablet, "Submitted · Undo" appears at the bottom left after you submit.
