## zh-CN

在平板、展开的折叠屏和横屏手机上，侧边栏的频道和私聊行更紧凑了（与网页一致），一屏能看到更多会话；“已保存”和“草稿”也换成与其他行相同的样式。桌面端侧边栏的分组标题更矮，行内文字改为常规粗细，频道名与网页对齐在同一条线上。

## en

On tablets, unfolded foldables and phones held sideways, channel and direct-message rows in the sidebar are more compact, matching the web, so more conversations fit on screen; Saved and Drafts now look like the other rows. On desktop, sidebar group headers are shorter, row names use a regular weight, and channel names line up with the web.
