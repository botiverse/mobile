## zh-CN

用鼠标或触控板时，侧栏服务器列表的展开 / 收起按钮和提醒里的消息链接标签现在也会显示悬停和按下状态，和网页版一致。

## en

With a mouse or trackpad, the expand / collapse button in the sidebar's server list and the message link tag on a reminder now also show hover and press states, as on the web.
