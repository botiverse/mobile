## zh-CN

对话框正文、小号字和副标题里的内容（数据、状态、步骤、正文）改为黑色正文字，更易读；只有解释后果、说明谁会看到或如何恢复的说明文字仍是灰色，与网页一致。

## en

Dialog body text, small text and subtitles that carry content (data, status, steps, prose) are now drawn in the main text colour for easier reading; only helper lines that explain a consequence, who sees something or how to recover stay grey, matching the web.

## ja
