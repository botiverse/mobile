## zh-CN

修复 iOS 手机端从服务面板进入「设置」或「成员」后返回主页没有过渡动画且无法侧滑返回的问题；现在这两个页面与其它子页一样原生推入导航栈，支持滑入/滑出动画和边缘侧滑返回。

## en

Fixed missing back transition animations and swipe-back gestures when returning from Settings or Members to Home on iPhone; both pages now push onto the native navigation stack with standard slide transitions and interactive swipe-back.

## ja
