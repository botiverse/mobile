✨ **New**
- App authorizations & permission review alerts: added **Settings › App authorizations** under the current server group (visible to every member, including guests) to manage app authorizations and link preview settings in one place. Server owners and admins now see a live attention dot on Apps when permission updates need review, and receive push notifications that open directly to the installed App's details.
- App card forms & encrypted secret inputs: App message card action buttons can now collect text and secret inputs right inside the confirmation dialog. Secret values are masked by default (with a visibility toggle) and encrypted on your device for that App alone so Raft cannot read them, alongside clear notices for unreviewed server-created Apps.
- Computer "Hand over" & removal guards: Computer details now label the creator as "Attached by" (updated across runtime sign-in and usage notices too) and add **Hand over** so the attacher or a server owner/admin can transfer a Computer to another owner or admin, with a 24-hour accept command, registrant details, Claude/Codex sign-out reminders, and cancel/decline controls for pending handovers. Leaving a server or removing a member with attached Computers now lists each affected Computer and its agent count—linking straight to delete them before leaving, or letting admins disconnect them and remove the member in one step.
- Native agent migration cards & preflight checks: tapping an agent migration action card in a conversation opens the native migration dialog with the suggested Computer preselected and marks the card complete once the move starts. Migration now checks target Computer runtime readiness beforehand—explaining any sign-in, usage-limit, or availability issue with a "Move anyway" option—and shows "Moved, can't respond" warnings on the agent profile and migration log if it still cannot reply afterwards.
- Upgraded in-app update experience: Google Play installs now support in-app updates (download in the background and tap "Restart to update", or update immediately for critical releases), while direct installs quietly pre-download newer releases on unmetered Wi-Fi with live download progress in Settings › About. Added a unified update prompt with 24-hour "Remind me later" snooze (replacing permanent version skip), a wide-screen top-bar update button, and a post-update "What's new" banner on Home.
- Close question cards directly with 1.5s undo: people asked a question card (or anyone eligible on an open question) can now close it without answering using the × button in the card header, with a 1.5-second "Closed · Undo" window before notifying the agent. Closed cards now display who closed them, and unrecognized states from newer servers safely appear as ended and read-only.
- Artifact Follow, wide split viewer & "Open in browser": follow or unfollow Artifacts directly from the Artifacts home and message card menus (unfollowing under "Relevant to me" keeps the item dimmed as "Unfollowed" with an Undo toast across refreshes). On wide screens, opening an Artifact from either Artifacts home or Activity displays it in the right split pane. The viewer also adds native Share link and restores "Open in browser", automatically closing standalone viewers opened from a conversation so returning to Raft lands right back in the chat.

🔧 **Improved**
- Cleaner phone conversation headers: on phones, channel, direct message, and Agent DM headers now fold to Back, avatar/icon, title (and subtitle), Search, and `⋯` so conversation titles have full room to read. Tasks, Files, Details & settings / Channel details & settings, and other conversation actions sit in `⋯`; tablet and wide layouts are unchanged.
- Real-time in-place Activity updates: Activity now aligns with the web—new messages and replies in followed threads update rows in place with per-row ordering so stale responses never roll rows back, cutting redundant full-list reloads and unread polls while refreshing faster via incremental sync on return from background.
- Shared external-channel drafts, guest server connect & thread sharing: external channels and their reply threads now share a single personal draft across participating servers—editing, sending, or clearing on one side updates or clears Drafts, the sidebar, Activity, and the composer on the other (system-shared drafts tied to one server stay preserved with a clear notice if sent from another). Channel guests can now use "Connect your servers" from Members and Channel details to connect an existing server, see which admin to ask, or "Create a server and connect"; private channels place "Connect your servers" under Channel settings, and inviting someone who is already a guest elsewhere sends a server-connect invite. External sharing now works directly into reply threads and uses the unified server switcher in the target picker.
- Clearer agent hold & Computer revocation recovery: when an agent cannot reply due to sign-in issues, usage limits, or rate limits, its profile, DM header, member lists, sidebar, and `@` mention picker now display "Can't respond" with the specific reason and reset time, automatically returning to live status once cleared. Revoked Computers now show "Key revoked" with the disconnect date, revocation cause, offline agent count, and steps to reattach or move agents, mirrored on each affected agent's profile. Aligned the built-in Computer minimum version to `1.0.53`, adding an npm/Homebrew uninstall-first note on older offline Computers and one-time reinstall or migration notices in the Home notification center for online or recently refused machines.
- Per-person Pro pricing & task hand-off messages: added support for server-resolved per-person Pro pricing across Plan & Billing, the upgrade dialog, agent creation, and seat-request cards—omitting non-seat agents from billing breakdowns, loading live server pricing, removing legacy seat blocks when creating agents, and completing seat-request cards directly when no extra seats are needed. Task hand-offs now render as actor messages with avatar, display name, and a hand-off card clearly distinguishing "Assigned to", "Assigned back to", and "Reassigned from … to …".
- Redesigned "Send to an agent" & web-parity polish: rebuilt the "Send to an agent" dialog to mirror a DM—agent picker in the header, read-only look-back of your last 5 messages, and the real chat composer below. Agent Activity now shows each sub-agent's model; agent-creation cards let you edit suggested non-secret environment variables (keeping config-directory variables unchecked by default with a warning); selecting an Antigravity Orchestrator model notes that mid-task replies may take longer; and external channels explain when a peer server disabled pinning instead of showing an unusable Pin button. Also aligned runtime detection retry, automatic runtime usage checks ("Check again" after a 2-minute cooldown), workspace refresh (pull-to-refresh on phones, icon button on wide screens), automatic Slack channel list loading, open-door Leave icons, and quiet fallback to latest messages when opening links to deleted messages.
- Tablet & landscape keyboard room and wide visual polish: sidebar channel and DM rows are more compact on tablets, unfolded foldables, and landscape phones so more conversations fit on screen, with more balanced wide touch header buttons. Opening the keyboard on tablets or landscape phones now hides the top bar (and conversation header if needed) to give typing more room, and on wide short screens where only the composer fits above the keyboard, the message box spans full width until the keyboard closes without disturbing your draft or cursor. Dialog body text, captions, and informational subtitles now use the primary text color for easier reading, and channel details row action buttons match the web's lighter sizing.
- Mouse/trackpad hover feedback & battery-friendly performance: with a mouse or trackpad, interactive controls across the app—including sidebar expand/collapse buttons, task headers and status chips, filter menus, reminder link tags, the Working now list, agent file trees, the web panel return strip, and reactions—now show consistent hover and press states matching the web, and the attachment comments panel header matches standard panel styling. Coalesced redundant channel and machine-status refreshes and batched link-card updates to reduce layout shifts when opening chats, and batched diagnostic trace uploads while pausing background upload timers to save battery.

- Fixed opening a channel or DM splicing disconnected message fragments into local history and showing a spurious "Loading older messages…" indicator mid-chat; fixed CJK sentences with two `$` signs (such as price ranges) being misparsed as inline math; kept qualified task reference chips intact on one line instead of breaking after hyphens or colons; kept App link card action buttons in a loading state with sibling buttons disabled until completion while tracking live status in the app's language; and fixed pending `@` mention banners below sent messages showing raw handles instead of display names.
- Fixed opening Tasks, Files, or channel details on a portrait tablet with the sidebar open pushing the Send button off screen (the sidebar now collapses automatically when space is tight and returns when you close the pane or rotate); fixed large phones in landscape squashing the composer in half when the keyboard opens so the Send button and toolbar stay visible; and fixed tall captures, custom-width sliders, or short/landscape viewports clipping the bottom "Copy Markdown" and "Download" buttons in the message screenshot share preview.
- Fixed the Home bottom tab bar occasionally being covered by overlapping layers and expanded each phone tab's vertical tap area while keeping its visual height and bottom system-gesture guard intact.
- Fixed subsequent newer releases staying masked behind an "Installed" status in Settings › About and not showing the Home update banner after completing a previous in-app update.
- Fixed non-interactive system maintenance exits on some Samsung and Xiaomi devices (such as post-install optimization or pre-launch cache cleanup) being misreported as unexpected crashes or triggering background-kill notices.
