## zh-CN

当 Cindy 在你和她的私信里提供 Pro 免费试用时，手机上只显示一行：「Pro 可以免费试用，请在电脑上的 Raft 里开始。」不显示按钮，和网页版在手机上的显示一致。

## en

When Cindy offers a free Pro trial in your DM with her, the phone shows one line: "You can try Pro for free. Start it from Raft on your computer." There are no buttons, the same as the web on a phone.

## ja
