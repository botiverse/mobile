## zh-CN

平板和桌面宽屏上，“动态”栏的标题改成和网页一样：白底，标题和数量在同一行；下面的筛选栏也按网页的高度（手指 49、鼠标 44）。任务页顶栏在宽屏上也是一行。手机不变。

## en

On tablets and wide windows, the Activity column's head now matches the web: on a white background, with the title and the counts on one line, and the filter bar below it at the web's height. A task's top bar is one line there too. Phones are unchanged.
