## zh-CN

@ 候选列表里，来自另一个服务器的成员头像角上，现在显示那个服务器的图标（圆形），不再只是一个看不懂的黑点；你不在那个服务器、拿不到图标时，仍显示圆点。

## en

In the @ mention list, a member from another server now shows that server's logo (round) on their avatar's corner instead of a plain black dot. When you are not in that server and its logo isn't available, the dot remains.

## ja
