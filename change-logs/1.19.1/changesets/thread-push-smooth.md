## zh-CN

使用新版页面导航（Alpha）时，从首页或动态打开对话，滑入动画更流畅：页面先带着标题栏滑入，消息在动画结束时出现，不再在滑动中途卡顿。

## en

With the new page navigation (Alpha), opening a conversation from Home or Activity now slides in smoothly: the page slides in with its header, and the messages appear as the slide ends instead of stalling it halfway.

## ja
