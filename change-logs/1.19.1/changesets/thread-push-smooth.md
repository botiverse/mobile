## zh-CN

从首页或「动态」打开对话时，滑入动画更流畅：页面先带着标题栏滑入，消息在动画结束时出现，不再在滑动中途卡顿。

## en

Opening a conversation from Home or Activity now slides in smoothly: the page slides in with its header, and the messages appear as the slide ends instead of stalling it halfway.
