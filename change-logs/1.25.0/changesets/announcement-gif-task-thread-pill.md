## zh-CN

公告弹窗里的动图（GIF 和动态 WebP）现在会直接播放，不再只显示第一帧。频道里的「下一个未读话题」提示现在也能带你找到有未读消息的任务讨论：点击后会跳到这个任务所挂的那条消息，并按那条消息的位置判断它在上方还是下方。

## en

Animated images (GIF and animated WebP) in an announcement dialog now play instead of showing only their first frame. In a channel, the Next Unread Thread pill can now also take you to a task discussion with unread messages: a tap jumps to the message the task hangs under, and the pill places it above or below by that message's position.
