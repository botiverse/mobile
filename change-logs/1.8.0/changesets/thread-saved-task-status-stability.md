## zh-CN

再次进入或刷新对话串时，已加载的收藏状态和任务状态会保持稳定，直到对应的共享状态源返回最新结果，不再短暂闪回默认状态。

## en

When reopening or refreshing a thread, loaded saved and task states now remain stable until their shared authorities return the latest result, instead of briefly flashing back to defaults.
