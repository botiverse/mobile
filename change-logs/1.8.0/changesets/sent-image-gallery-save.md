## zh-CN

已发送的图片现在可以直接保存到 Android、iOS 和 HarmonyOS 系统相册；重复保存会生成独立图片，取消、权限拒绝或类型校验失败时不会影响下一次保存。

## en

Sent images can now be saved directly to the system photo library on Android, iOS, and HarmonyOS. Repeated saves create independent photos, while cancellation, permission denial, or type-validation failures do not affect the next save.
