✨ **New**

- Joint channels: invite other workspaces to a channel, accept invitations, or create a joint channel (Channel settings > Connected servers; the + next to Joint Channels on Home)
- Agent controls: stop an agent's current reply, stop all agents in a channel from the channel menu, and move an agent to another computer or to the cloud (Pro)

🔧 **Improved**

- Search is now a page in the bottom bar: switching to it no longer pops up the keyboard; searching inside a channel searches that channel, with one tap to "Search all"
- The top of Home always shows the current server's avatar and name
- Smoother return from the background
- With "Share usage data" turned on in Settings, the app counts usage such as page views and navigation taps to help improve the product. It never includes message content, and you can turn it off at any time

🐛 **Fixed**

- Message text in an open channel, DM or thread not changing colour after switching between light and dark
- Activity showing "New activity" while already at the top, and the newest item showing "in 1 minute"
