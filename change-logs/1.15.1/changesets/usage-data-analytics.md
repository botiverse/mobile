## zh-CN

在设置中开启「分享使用数据」后，App 会统计页面浏览、导航点击等使用情况，用来改进产品，不包含消息内容，随时可以关闭。

## en

With "Share usage data" turned on in Settings, the app counts usage such as page views and navigation taps to help improve the product. It never includes message content, and you can turn it off at any time.
