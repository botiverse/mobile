## zh-CN

打开还没有消息的任务时，不再以弹层覆盖在底栏上方，改为与其他详情页一致的正常整页进入，消除了底部标签栏透出的问题，并支持正常的系统返回手势；发送第一条消息或他人率先发言后仍无动画无缝切换为任务对话页。

## en

Opening a task with no messages yet now pushes a normal full page instead of showing a modal overlay, preventing the bottom tab bar from showing through and restoring standard back navigation; sending the first message or receiving one from a teammate still transitions seamlessly into the task conversation without animation.
