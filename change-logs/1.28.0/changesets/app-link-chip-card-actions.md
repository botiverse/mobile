## zh-CN
- 点开消息里的应用链接后，卡片上现在会显示该应用提供的按钮（例如合并、批准），刷新和其他链接收在「⋯」更多菜单里，和网页端一致；此前只有「打开链接」。

## en
- The card that opens from an App link in a message now shows the App's own buttons (such as Merge and Approve), with Refresh and the other links under the ⋯ More menu, as on the web. Before, it only offered Open link.
