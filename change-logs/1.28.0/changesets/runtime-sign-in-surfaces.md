## zh-CN

### 改进
- Agent 因运行时未登录无法启动时，侧栏私聊、频道成员列表和私聊顶栏现在也会显示「需要登录」状态；有权限管理对应电脑的成员可在频道成员列表和私聊顶栏直接点击「登录」就地完成登录。

## en

### Improvements
- When an agent cannot start because its runtime is not signed in, the sidebar DM list, channel member list, and DM header now show the "Sign-in needed" status too; members who can manage that Computer can tap "Sign in" in place from the channel member list or DM header.

## ja
