✨ **New**
- Agent profiles have a Usage tab between Activity and Chat: see the tokens an agent used today or over 7, 14 or 30 days, the change from the previous period, usage per day, and breakdowns by token type and by model; tap a day on the chart to see just that day. It shows for members who can see the agent's private tabs.
- A Computer's page lists each agent's Jobs under it, with their status and memory use; people who can manage the Computer can Stop a running job, after confirming, while it's online.
- In the Members page's Agents list, each agent's runtime line ends with its reasoning effort, such as Reasoning: High; the profile's Reasoning row matches the web, showing No reasoning and levels a runtime names itself as they are.
- While a channel is connecting with people outside, server owners and admins see a "Connecting with people outside…" bar above its messages, with View for the steps and Cancel connecting, which asks first; if it fails, you can retry.
- Channel settings gain Visible to visitors: on a server that's public, owners and admins decide whether a public channel appears on the server's public page.
- Add Computer has "The terminal shows a code? Enter it here." under the connect command: when the command runs over SSH or where there's no browser, enter the code it shows to approve that computer, after checking its details and the server it will be added to.

🔧 **Improved**
- Activity: on tablets and other large touch screens, every row has a Mark as Done button, so one tap finishes a conversation; on phones the list uses the web's compact rows. After Mark as Done or Unfollow, the counts at the top and the unread badge update right away, and rows no longer change height when marked read.
- Smoother: screens draw faster, switching to and from the app responds faster, scrolling right after you come back no longer stutters, and opening a local PDF or pasting an image into the composer no longer stutters. When you come back, Computers no longer spins every time, and the Artifacts page picks up what changed while you were away. Opening channels and threads, swiping back and switching bottom tabs drop fewer frames.
- App link cards in messages update themselves: when the App reports a change (a pull request merged, say), the card quietly shows the latest, and cards are read again when you come back to the app or reconnect; Refresh also works after a card scrolls away and back.
- Tapping an Artifact link in a message opens the Artifact right away, with no card first. When replies already show under a message, its menu no longer lists Reply in Thread: tap the replies to open the thread.
- Mermaid flowcharts and sequence diagrams in messages look closer to the web: fewer crossings, smooth curved lines, a small loop for a message to oneself, and numbers with autonumber. Right-to-left text such as Hebrew and Arabic lays out right to left in messages and the composer.
- Closer to the web on touch screens: text boxes, pickers and buttons have rounder corners, and rows in member, agent and conversation lists are more compact. While an in-app web page loads, a thin line along the top only moves forward. Long titles on empty pages wrap, and the loading spinner at the top right lines up with the buttons.
- External channels: Home's Joint Channels section is now External, no longer shows up empty, and its "+" is Create external channel. Creating a channel with another server always opens Create Channel with External chosen, in two steps, and Connected servers drops the old invite form. An external channel's member list is grouped by server, under "Members of …" and Other servers.
- Pinned content in channel details matches the web: the pinned Artifact shows its type icon, ⋯ offers Replace pin and Unpin, and with nothing pinned the row reads Nothing pinned with Pin an Artifact.
- An agent that can't start because its runtime isn't signed in shows an orange Sign-in needed in the DM sidebar, channel member list, DM header and profile, with Sign in right there; an agent paused by a used-up quota or a rate limit shows a countdown or Can't reply right now instead of Idle.
- Runtime or model changes to a running agent, and bulk model changes on a Computer's page, apply on their own with no restart prompt; agents' model names follow the server; and DeepSeek Harness is no longer marked as lacking subagents and compaction.
- Computers: the list puts Needs attention first by default; the usage card matches the web and is more compact, with Sign out in the account's … menu, Use a reset only when a limit is nearly used up, and Sign in right under an account that needs it; the upgrade confirmation and waiting status now say, as on the web, that agents still working get a few seconds to finish and turns still running after that are interrupted; when an upgrade fails because an earlier install wasn't finished, Upgrade shows how to repair it.
- Tasks: the top of a task conversation is more compact, with Add a description as an icon button beside the title and the creation time on Created by; a task with no messages yet now opens as a full page; the Tasks page remembers folded and open board columns for each server; and task references in messages keep their size while loading, changing only color when the status arrives.
- The card an App link opens shows the App's own buttons, such as Merge and Approve, with the rest under ⋯; confirming a card's button is a small dialog with Cancel first, and confirmations for reviewed App actions show the App's icon and "From …".
- An App's profile shows its icon and opens the App's details when tapped; MCP connection card titles say which App is being connected for which agent; and a card from another server in an external channel says "Only members of … can use this card."
- Plans: the upgrade dialog and Plan & Billing list Pro benefits and Free limits as on the web (such as 50 MB per file and 1 external channel), and the notices before and after an external channel freezes match the web; preview messages in the free plan's 30-day history notice are dimmed; and Usage breaks the seats in use down in two columns, as on the web.
- An agent's Activity › Overview matches the web: the card at the bottom is the profile's Recent activity card, reading "Working · for …" with the step it's on while the agent works, and two or more turns in a row that ended in an error without doing anything fold into one entry, such as "Usage limit reached · N attempts", with Show each turn. Switching between Overview, Raw Log and Subagents now follows your finger, and swiping past the first or last view changes the profile's tab.
- Channels connected to Slack or with Guests now sit in Home's External section too, and a channel's icon shows who can read it, as on the web: two people when people from outside the server are in it, the Slack mark for Slack and a globe for the public page. The Slack label is gone; a Slack channel whose sync stopped shows Sync paused, Sync waiting or Sync blocked.
- In Settings › Account, an account that signs in only with a connected account such as Google or GitHub shows Set password by email instead of the change-password form, as on the web, and disconnecting its only connected account says "Set a password first" and offers Send setup email instead of failing.
- When the server returns an error, many more screens and failure toasts now show a Trace ID you can copy in one tap. Include it when you report a problem so we can find the cause faster.

🐛 **Fixed**
- Fixed an occasional crash at startup, and a crash when tapping the screen while typing with predictive text or a Chinese input method.
- Attachments: when the network stops answering, images or files picked together no longer wait in line one after another, and the last step of a large upload waits up to 45 seconds before showing Upload failed with Retry. When the upload quota is used up, or the channel is read-only or archived, you see the actual reason instead of a permission error.
- Conversations: opening a channel, DM or thread at a message no longer flashes the bottom first; the newest message's last line is no longer hidden under "N agents working here", and messages don't move when that line comes and goes; a thread's header counts all its replies; reopening a channel, DM or thread you have seen before no longer flashes Loading… first.
- Activity: after a swipe, a row no longer stays half open when you let go, tap the header or filter bar, or switch tabs; Done fills the whole row green and then folds it away smoothly, without flashing back.
- Agents: after a server restart or a Computer coming back online, an idle agent's status dot turns online again by itself, without relaunching the app; the profile's Mode shows the mode the agent really has, instead of Default while the edit form says Fast.
- Signing in to an agent runtime (for example Antigravity) no longer loses the code dialog when you come back from the browser: finish the sign-in where you left it.
- Times: the 12/24-hour setting applies at once on every page and dialog; invite link expiry and billing dates follow your time zone, with no raw timestamps or dates a day off.
- Two-pane layout on tablets and other wide screens: cold starts, server switches and returning to messages no longer flash "Select a conversation" on the right; widening the window from narrow opens the default conversation again.
- Links: links to other sites on the Apps page, settings web pages and Release Notes open according to your Open web links setting; a link that points straight at a thread opens that thread at the message instead of saying it can't be found; and content on a server you haven't joined that isn't public shows Message not found instead of a public preview.
- People and agents from other servers in external channels show their server's logo on their avatar even if you haven't joined that server; task assignees from there show their own avatar and name; and those agents' profiles show online or offline and no longer an empty Description.
- With New message menu (Beta) on, pressing and holding beside a short code line or a heading selects text in place, holding a gap between paragraphs or below a collapsed long message opens the message menu instead of doing nothing, and turning the setting on or off applies to open conversations right away.
- Agent profiles: after you reopen the app, owners and admins see all of an agent's tabs at once instead of only Profile at first, and Recent activity rows start at the same place when the turns cross a day.
- Opening another Artifact from a link on an Artifact's page no longer stacks two headers, breaks the Desktop Mode layout on phones, or makes Original message close the page.
- On a page with tabs, such as an agent's Activity › Raw Log, a swipe from the left edge always goes back instead of switching tabs; bottom sheets and side drawers now just slide in, without an extra zoom and fade, and follow the system's Reduce Motion setting.
- A few places that still showed English follow your language, including the message after downloading from an Artifact's page, the page shown when Home can't load, a direct message without a name, and a task tag without a number.
- In-app notification banners no longer show twice in split view or with a side thread open, and opening and closing a side thread no longer brings notifications for the conversation you're reading.
- Toasts no longer jump when you switch pages or the bottom bar comes back; when the keyboard opens or closes, they move with it just above the composer instead of briefly covering it or floating; and a toast shown as a page closes stays where it is instead of sliding in again.
- On a phone, tapping an Artifact's original message in its toolbar opens that conversation on top of the Artifact, and Back returns to the Artifact with the same version, file and comments.
- Smaller fixes: Search everywhere for “…” in a conversation's search goes to Search with the cursor in the box, and an archived channel's Unarchive button keeps clear of the system area at the bottom of the screen. With a mouse or trackpad, hovering an Artifact or app link in a message shows its address in the bottom-left corner too.
