## zh-CN

平板、折叠屏和横屏（窗口宽 840dp 起，桌面模式开着）：主页列表不再重复左侧导航栏已有的"搜索"和"动态"两行，只保留"收藏"；从左侧导航栏打开的搜索和收藏列表里，右侧正在显示的会话对应的那一行会保持选中高亮，和主页、动态列表一样，右侧会话关闭后高亮随之消失。

## en

Tablets, foldables and landscape (windows from 840dp with desktop mode on): the Home list no longer repeats the Search and Activity rows that the left rail already has, and keeps only Saved. In the Search and Saved lists opened from the rail, the row whose conversation is open on the right stays highlighted, as in the Home and Activity lists, and the highlight goes once that conversation closes.
