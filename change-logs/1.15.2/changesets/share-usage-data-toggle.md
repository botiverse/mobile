## zh-CN

设置 › 账户新增「共享使用数据」开关（与 Web 一致）：可以随时在手机上开启或关闭；从未选择过的账户显示为开启。关闭后，App 会立即停止发送使用数据。「了解更多」打开隐私政策。保存失败时开关会恢复原状并提示重试。该开关随服务端功能开关逐步开放。

## en

Settings › Account now has a "Share usage data" switch (same as on the web), so you can turn it on or off from your phone. Accounts that never chose show it as on. Turning it off stops the app from sending usage data right away. "Learn more" opens the privacy policy. If saving fails, the switch goes back and asks you to try again. The switch rolls out with a server feature flag.
