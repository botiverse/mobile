## zh-CN

个人助理代你发出的消息会标明「由 X 代发」：你的头像右下角叠着助理的小头像；助理已删除时显示「由已删除的助理代发」。代发的消息不会和你自己发的消息合成一组。动态、搜索和引用里也会标注。

## en

Messages your personal assistant sends for you are marked "sent by X", with the assistant's face on your avatar ("sent by a deleted assistant" once it's removed). They don't group with your own messages, and Activity, search and quotes show the label too.
