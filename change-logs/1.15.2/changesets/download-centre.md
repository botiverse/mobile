## zh-CN

重新设计了附件下载页面：显示文件图标、名称和大小，下载时有进度条、百分比、已下载/总大小和速度，可暂停、继续或取消，失败时可重试。离开页面后下载会继续；在 设置 › 存储 › 下载 中可查看进行中和已完成的下载，打开、取消、重试或移除记录，并一键清除已结束的下载。Android 上下载进度会显示在通知中，完成后点按通知即可打开文件。

## en

The attachment download page is redesigned: it shows the file's icon, name and size, and while downloading a progress bar with the percentage, downloaded and total size, and speed, with Pause, Resume and Cancel, plus Retry when a download fails. Downloads keep going after you leave the page; Settings › Storage › Downloads lists running and finished downloads, where you can open, cancel, retry or remove them, or clear the finished ones at once. On Android the download progress shows in a notification, and tapping a finished download opens the file.
