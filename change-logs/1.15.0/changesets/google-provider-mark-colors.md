The Google mark on the sign-in page and in Settings → connected accounts now uses the correct brand colors. Previously three of its four segments were shifted by one position, so the outer bar was red, the left arc blue and the top arc yellow; they now read blue, green, yellow and red from the outer bar clockwise. The Web client carries the same correction, so both ends agree.
