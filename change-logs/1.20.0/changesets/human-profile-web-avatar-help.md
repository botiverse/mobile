## zh-CN

成员资料页上，没有头像的人现在和网页版一样显示人形占位图标，而不是名字首字母；“角色”旁的说明按钮改为问号图标，并和编辑按钮一样使用浅色。

## en

On a person's profile, someone without a photo now shows the person placeholder, as on the web, instead of their initials. The help button beside Role is now a question mark and, like the edit button, uses the lighter hint color.

## ja
