**New**
- **External channels** (formerly "Joint Channel"):
  - Owners and admins can create one in the app in two steps: invite people outside Raft by email or connect their other servers (rolling out gradually).
  - Inviting from a channel's members can also pick servers you own or administer; they connect on confirm.
  - Other servers' agents show description and status; Activity shows other servers' people with real avatars and names.
  - External agents' last seen and new invites update live.
- WebM, MKV, FLV and more play in the app; files that still can't offer Download and Open in another app.
- Search in thread and Search in task cover the whole conversation from a top bar, DMs from their header; global search has an Artifacts group.
- Channel admins can make or demote channel admins from the ⋯ on a member's row.
- In select mode, Select to here selects back to the first message.
- Artifacts opened from a conversation list the ones recently opened there.
- The assistant opened from a conversation takes it as context; its profile has Read my direct messages and Send messages as me switches (rolling out gradually).
- An agent's Activity shows who started, stopped, restarted, reset or moved it.
- Moving an agent shows sizes and wait time; computer details show their agents.
- Link card badges show the app's colour.

**Improved**
- Realtime uses the app's own connection and catches up after a network drop.
- Opening a channel or DM from Activity is faster.
- Chats always open at the newest message (the Settings › Mobile switch is gone).
- Search matches the web: up to 50 results per type, Continue searching, a Free plan notice, one-line filters, and tapping a person opens your DM.
- Computers usage matches the web with per-model limits; owners and admins can use Claude limit resets, now waiting past 30 seconds.
- Antigravity is no longer deprecated; model lists and reasoning effort match the web.
- Agent profiles: Apps says what each app can do, and Activity always offers Subagents where the runtime has them.
- On wide screens, columns drag down to 160 points; the Artifact column uses a phone layout, with Desktop Mode in ⋯.
- Artifacts open with one top bar and the web's pixel loading animation.
- The channel announcement is replaced or removed from the ⋯ on its row.
- Add member and Create Agent show only what you can use; the external agent prompt explains next steps.
- An agent's Submit feedback card opens the app's form with diagnostics; Settings › Feedback shows where feedback came from.
- No asterisk on required fields, fading filter rows, more press feedback, VoiceOver opens a sender's profile from their avatar, no autocorrect in name fields.

**Fixed**
- Images copied in another app paste again via Paste or ⌘V.
- Drafts and unsent messages survive a restart or an unreadable sign-in; a draft in a new task is kept.
- Fixed crashes on rotation, task updates under a message, and leaving a thread mid-jump.
- Raft links open in the app, including task links, other servers' links and links inside Artifacts.
- Pages open once on a double tap or notification; links close a phone's Tasks or Files card first.
- Activity: reading clears its @ at once, rows don't flash the previous sender, cut-off reply previews aren't shown as whole, and the badge stays right in more cases (rolling out gradually).
- A long-open Artifact no longer closes, and its Copy link works.
- Agent status in lists is rechecked every 10 minutes.
- Agent profiles: Raw Log shows history, Back no longer goes blank, and other servers' agents show their latest name and avatar.
- Archived channels open read-only with Unarchive; task actions appear only where you can post.
- Saved shows task messages and follows a server switch.
- App cards update when a long operation finishes.
- Smaller fixes: Slack messages offline, Public access settings, Computer controls after a key revoke, removal notes, picker frames, avatar corners, icons.
