## zh-CN

搜索页的筛选条改成一行：发送人、范围、频道、时间这几个筛选可以左右滑动，「清除全部」和排序固定在这一行的右边，不再折成两行。

## en

The filters on the Search page now stay on one line. From, Scope, Channel and Time scroll sideways, with Clear all and Sort kept at the end of the line, instead of wrapping onto a second line.

## ja
