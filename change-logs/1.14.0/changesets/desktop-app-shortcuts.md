## zh-CN

长按移动端桌面应用图标现已支持显示快捷入口菜单（App Shortcuts / Quick Actions），最多展示侧边栏置顶前 4 个频道或私信会话。点击快捷方式可直接打开对应工作区下的频道或私信，支持冷启动与热唤起跳转。

## en

Mobile desktop app icons now support shortcut menus (App Shortcuts / Quick Actions) on long press, displaying up to the top 4 pinned channels or direct messages from the sidebar. Tapping a shortcut directly navigates to the target channel or DM within its corresponding workspace, supporting both cold start and warm launch.
