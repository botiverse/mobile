## zh-CN
应用内浏览器（已安装的 Raft App）与 Artifact 查看器现统一支持全屏 ↔ 可拖拽缩放悬浮小窗 ↔ 左右磁吸侧边胶囊三态切换：在浮窗模式下可自由拖动并在左下角/右下角双向缩放尺寸，浮窗外区域完全透传可继续在底层阅读和回复会话；支持一键收起到左右边缘胶囊、一键展开回全屏（复用原保活 WebView 零重载）及跨 App 与 Artifact 的统一多页切换器，退出登录或切换账号/服务器/环境时自动清空所有浮窗与保活实例。

## en
Installed Raft Apps and the Artifact Viewer now share a unified Full-Screen ↔ Resizable Mini-Window ↔ Bilateral Edge-Docked Pill workflow. Floating either surface opens an interactive mini-window that can be freely dragged across safe viewport bounds and resized from both bottom-left and bottom-right corners while keeping underlying chat/home touches fully pass-through. Users can collapse to the left/right magnetic edge pill, expand back to full screen with zero WebView reload by reusing the warm keep-alive slot, switch across minimized Apps and Artifacts from a unified switcher sheet, and all floating windows and warm WebViews clear immediately on logout or account/server/environment switches.

## ja
