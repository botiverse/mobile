## zh-CN
应用内浏览器的磁吸悬浮胶囊现支持跨屏自由拖动并吸附到屏幕左侧或右侧边缘：默认停靠在右侧，拖动时以完整圆角胶囊跟手移动，松手后按距离自动平滑吸附至左侧或右侧并贴边收口，同时完整保留安全区、软键盘与语音通话卡片避让以及挂起/恢复后的停靠位置记忆。

## en
The in-app browser's magnetic floating tab pill can now be dragged freely across the viewport and docked flush to either the left or right screen edge (defaulting to Right). While dragged or settling, the pill switches to a fully rounded capsule, snaps smoothly to the closer edge on release (or instantly with Reduced Motion), respects safe areas, keyboard, and active voice-call insets, and remembers its docked edge across suspend/resume and tab switches.
