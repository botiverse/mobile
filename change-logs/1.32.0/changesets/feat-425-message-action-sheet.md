## zh-CN

重构移动端消息长按操作体验：长按消息正文不再与文本选区冲突，统一拉起可多档拖拽调节高度（紧凑 / 展开 / 全屏）的底部操作抽屉，支持同抽屉原地切换「选择文本」与表情选择器、实时同步选中字符数并直接引用回复或复制选中内容。

## en

Redesigned mobile message long-press actions with a multi-detent resizable bottom sheet (Compact, Expanded, Full) that eliminates body selection conflicts, morphs in place for Select Text and the full emoji picker, and live-updates Copy/Quote selection actions as native selection handles move.
