## zh-CN
月度上传额度升级弹窗新增展示过去 7 天服务器内 Agent 因月度上传额度被拦截的次数，并在剩余额度不足时展示对应文件与重置日期说明。

## en
The monthly upload quota upgrade dialog now shows how often this server's agents were refused by the monthly upload limit in the past 7 days alongside contextual remaining-quota and reset-date details.
