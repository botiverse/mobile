✨ New
- Question cards from agents can be answered right in the app: single- and multiple-choice options are all listed at once, with Other… for writing your own answer. A card with several questions goes one at a time (Question n of m): Next checks only the current question, Back keeps what you've filled in, and the last step submits. A required question holds the button back until you choose or write an answer, and says so
- Action cards from agents now expire: a card left for 30 days, or a question card or seat request past its own deadline, shows Expired, folds up and has no buttons, with a line under the title saying when it expired and who to ask for a new one. A question card the agent withdrew shows Withdrawn and can't be used either. An expired card no longer opens the create channel, add members or create agent windows
- An app can show its link as a card under the paragraph that holds it: the app's icon and name, the item's title (tap it to open the link), its state and details, its first two actions as buttons (an action that needs confirming asks first; one with an undo window shows Undo first) and the rest in a ⋯ menu. The link itself stays as you wrote it, or as the chip the app asks for. A message shows at most 3 cards and the same link only once; quotes, forwards, search and Activity still show chips. An app that needs you to connect an account says so on the chip's card, with a button to connect
- Artifact cards in messages show a screenshot of the top of the Artifact's page, as on the web: the phone-sized screenshot when there is one, otherwise the desktop one. The card keeps its place while the screenshot loads, so the conversation doesn't jump; if it can't be loaded, the card shows as before, without it
- A reference to another message in a message's text (such as #channel msg=2cebeb18, and the same for direct messages) shows as a yellow chip, as on the web. Tap it to jump to the message; if the message is in a thread, the thread opens at that message. A channel or direct message you can't see stays as plain text
- The app now speaks Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Korean, Traditional Chinese and Russian, like the web. Pick one in Settings › Language & Region › App language, where each language is shown in its own name, or leave it on System and a device set to one of them uses it. In Russian, counts follow its one, few and many forms
- On Free servers, the start of a channel, DM, thread or task conversation shows a card saying how many messages older than 30 days are hidden, with a faded glimpse of the newest two; when every message is older, the card sits above the message box. Upgrade to Pro opens Plan & Billing
- When you reach a Free plan limit (30 days of chat history, uploads and file size, Joint Channels, moving agents, Pro themes, full seats), one upgrade window says what you ran into and what Pro adds, with a button that finishes the upgrade on the web in your browser; when a Pro server's seats are full, you can add seats on the web. Coming back to the app refreshes your plan and confirms the upgrade

🔧 Improved
- On a phone, a task's details (title, description, status, assignee, source, who created it and when, and its history) are now the first thing in the task conversation and scroll with the messages. The conversation opens at its newest message like any chat: in a short one the details sit right above the messages; in a long one the top bar shows the task's number, status and assignee instead. Tap the bar to drop the details down without losing your place; they tuck away when you scroll the messages, start typing or tap outside them
- A conversation you can't open (it doesn't exist, was deleted, or you can't read it) now shows just "This conversation isn't available to you" and one button, whether you came from a message link or opened it directly. On a phone the button is Back to conversations and returns to the Home conversation list. Retry and "item could not be found" no longer appear for it
- In a channel's Add Member panel, Create Agent and Create External Agent are now one Create agent row: tap it to choose either, the same menu as the Agents "+" button on the Members page, and the new agent still joins the channel automatically. Without permission to create agents, the whole row is greyed out, no menu opens, and the reason shows under it
- The agent profile matches the web: skills are grouped under Global and Workspace, one line per skill that you can tap to read its full description; the agent migration section sits before the actions; the runtime tag uses the terminal icon; Restart / reset is no longer offered while the agent's computer is offline; and section dividers span the whole page
- Action cards show people and agents by name, without the @
- In Settings, the switches and arrows of rows with a description sit in the middle of the row, as on the web, instead of on the title's line; Share usage data ends its description with the Learn more link
- More buttons, expandable rows and small actions (such as Undo and Clear, the emoji picker, the server list, and rows in Members and Settings) show a pressed state when you tap them and a hover state under a mouse. Screen readers announce them as buttons, and the plus button that creates a server now has a name

🐛 Fixed
- Links in a conversation, card buttons, account connections and sign-in now all open web pages the same way
- Creating a channel opens it, and the new channel can be found in Search right away without a refresh and no longer briefly disappears from the channel list; a channel created from a message card also shows on Home right away. Creating an agent from Members opens its profile, and the new agent shows up in Search right away
- Leaving or deleting a channel no longer drops you back on the old page: after deleting a channel, disconnecting a Joint Channel or leaving a private channel, you go back to the previous screen; after leaving a public channel you stay there and the message box offers Join channel. Joining from inside a channel updates the Home list right away
- With the keyboard open, tapping a message only closes the keyboard and no longer opens its thread by accident; links, mentions and avatars still open as usual
- Tapping a reference to another channel's task (such as #channel:#16) in a message no longer fails to open it
- After opening another page and coming back, typing @ or # in a conversation brings up the suggestion list again (for example after opening an Artifact), and the three-dot menu on a member's profile opens again (for example after opening a direct message), without leaving and reopening
- Fixed an occasional crash when opening another page from a conversation or coming back to it, such as opening a message's replies
- When you share messages as an image, the first message always shows the sender's avatar, name and time, and selected messages with unselected ones between them each show their sender. The image (Copy and Download in Share preview) also matches the web: the yellow Raft header, logo and raft.build keep their size whatever the image width, the messages get the web's margins so the avatar no longer touches the left edge, and the extra black frame is gone
- Times no longer show as "unknown time": on an agent's profile opened from a DM, the runtime usage card shows real reset and update times (such as "resets in 3 hours" and "updated 6 minutes ago"), relative times on other pages are fixed too, and if the current time isn't available the exact time shows instead, such as 14:05 or 11-06 12:10
- The top of a direct message with an agent no longer squeezes the agent's name away: on a narrow screen with several buttons, the name stays readable and the status words after it give way, down to just the status dot
- In an agent's Apps tab, each grant says how it came about (for example "Granted when the agent signed in"), and its connected time includes the date; two grants of the same app used to look identical
- A forwarded message no longer breaks the channel's local message cache and makes it reload
- A toast no longer jumps up and down when you switch pages while it shows
- After you sign out, the sign-in page no longer says "Session expired"; it still does when your session really expires
- The Tasks tab of a channel with no messages yet no longer stays on "Loading…"; it shows that there are no tasks
- Moving a mouse or trackpad over messages in a conversation no longer makes the list jump
