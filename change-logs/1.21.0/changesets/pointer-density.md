## zh-CN

在桌面版和使用触控板或鼠标时，界面更紧凑：输入框、按钮、标签页、菜单项和侧边栏的行统一为同一高度；页面和面板的标题栏更矮，返回和标题栏里的按钮更小；顶栏更矮，左侧导航栏更窄；开关更小；设置里的卡片留白更宽松、间距更大。用手指操作时，一切保持原样。

## en

On the desktop app and with a trackpad or mouse, the app is more compact: fields, buttons, tabs, menu items and sidebar rows share one height; page and panel heads are shorter, with smaller Back and head buttons; the top bar is shorter and the side rail narrower; switches are smaller; and settings cards get more room inside and more space between them. With a finger, everything stays as it was.

## ja
