## zh-CN

从私信打开 Agent 资料页时，运行时用量卡片里的重置时间和“更新于”都显示成“未知时间”。现在会正确显示，比如“3 小时后重置”“6 分钟前更新”。

## en

On an agent's profile opened from a DM, the runtime usage card showed "unknown time" for every reset and for when it was last updated. It now shows real times, such as "resets in 3 hours" and "updated 6 minutes ago".

## ja
