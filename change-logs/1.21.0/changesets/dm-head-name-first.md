## zh-CN

和 Agent 的私信顶部不会再把对方名字挤没了。顶部按钮多、屏幕窄时，名字先保证显示，后面的状态文字往后让，最窄时只留状态圆点。

## en

The top of a direct message with an agent no longer squeezes the agent's name away. When the screen is narrow and the top has several buttons, the name stays readable and the status words after it give way, down to just the status dot.

## ja
