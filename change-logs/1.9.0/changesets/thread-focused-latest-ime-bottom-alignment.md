## zh-CN

从动态定位到 Thread 最新消息后打开键盘时，最新对话内容现在会保持在输入框上方并贴合底部，不再只剩一条消息顶对齐和大片空白。定位历史消息和用户主动滚动的行为保持不变。

## en

When the keyboard opens after focusing the latest Thread message from Activity, the latest conversation now stays visible and bottom-aligned above the composer instead of leaving a single top-aligned row and a large blank area. Focused history messages and direct user scrolling keep their existing behavior.
