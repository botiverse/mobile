## zh-CN

输入框中的 `@` 或 `#` 候选列表现在会在键盘显示、隐藏以及窗口尺寸变化时继续贴合输入框移动；无需关闭重开，候选的可见位置与点击区域也会保持一致。

## en

Open `@` and `#` suggestions now stay attached to the composer when the keyboard opens or closes and when the window size changes. The list no longer needs to be reopened, and each candidate's visible position stays aligned with its tap target.
