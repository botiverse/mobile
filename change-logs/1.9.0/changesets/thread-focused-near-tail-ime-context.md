## zh-CN

从动态定位到接近对话末尾的消息后打开键盘时，只要当前消息到最新上下文能够完整显示，它们现在会一起保持在输入框上方，不再出现大片空白；较长的历史上下文和用户主动滚动仍保持原有行为。

## en

When the keyboard opens after focusing a message near the end of a Thread, the focused message and recent context now stay together above the composer whenever that tail fits, avoiding a large blank area. Longer history context and direct user scrolling keep their existing behavior.
