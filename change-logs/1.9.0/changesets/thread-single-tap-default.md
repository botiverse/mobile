## zh-CN

未设置过偏好的用户默认不会通过单击消息正文打开话题；仍可在设置中开启该功能，开启后仅已有回复的消息支持单击打开。已明确关闭或开启的用户会继续保留原有选择。

## en

For people who have not set a preference, tapping a message body no longer opens its thread by default. The behavior remains available in Settings and, when enabled, applies only to messages with existing replies. Existing explicit choices to disable or enable it remain unchanged.
