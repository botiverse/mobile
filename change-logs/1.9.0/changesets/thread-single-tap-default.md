## zh-CN

未设置过偏好的用户现在单击对话中的普通消息即可直接打开对应话题；已明确关闭或开启“单击打开话题”的用户会继续保留原有选择。

## en

For people who have not set a preference, a single tap on a regular message now opens its thread directly. Existing explicit choices to disable or enable “Single tap opens Thread” remain unchanged.
