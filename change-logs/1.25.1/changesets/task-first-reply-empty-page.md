## zh-CN

在还没有讨论的任务里发出第一条回复后，任务的讨论页面会直接显示出来，不再被留在上面的空白页面（仍带着刚发出的文字）挡住，返回也能正常离开。

## en

After you send the first reply in a task with no discussion yet, its conversation now shows right away. The empty page no longer stays on top with your sent text still in the message box, and Back works again.
