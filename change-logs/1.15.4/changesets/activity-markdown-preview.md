## zh-CN

动态里的标题和预览不再露出 `**`、反引号这类 markdown 符号，加粗、代码、链接照网页一样显示；搜索、收藏、通知等处的预览也统一成干净的文字。

## en

Activity titles and previews no longer show raw Markdown such as `**` or backticks; bold, code and links display like on the web, and previews in search, saved items and notifications read as clean text.
