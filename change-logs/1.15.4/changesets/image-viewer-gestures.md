## zh-CN

看图更顺手：双击放大到点的位置，再双击复原；双指缩放以两指中间为中心；左右划时下一张跟着手指滑进来；往下划关闭看图；放大后拖到图片边缘就停住，继续拖切到下一张；前后几张原图提前加载，切换时直接是清晰的。

## en

Image viewing feels better: double-tap zooms in where you tap and double-tap again resets; pinch zooms around your fingers; the next image slides in under your finger as you swipe; swipe down to close; a zoomed image stops at its edges and keeps going to the next image; neighbouring images load ahead, so they're sharp when you get there.
