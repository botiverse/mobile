✨ New
- Long-press to copy in many more places: channel names and descriptions, member names, account details, join links, conversation titles, task numbers and titles, file names, and more. What you copy is exactly what's shown
- Settings › Account shows your connected accounts, and you can disconnect them
- Tasks, Computers, channel members, files and more support pull to refresh

🔧 Improved
- Better image viewing: double-tap to zoom, pinch zooms around your fingers, swipe sideways to change images, swipe down to close, and a zoomed image stops at its edges
- The interface follows the web's latest design: wide-screen layout and sidebar, the member list, action cards in messages, notices, and dialog buttons
- Add Computer opens from the bottom of the screen
- The file page shows download progress
- Join links in server settings show the full URL, so you can share what you copy as is

🐛 Fixed
- Previews in Activity, Search, Saved and notifications no longer show Markdown symbols such as `**` or backticks
- After switching servers, Search no longer shows the previous server's channels and people
- The Create Joint Channel dialog no longer opens empty
- Task status menus and status chips in messages follow the app language instead of showing English
