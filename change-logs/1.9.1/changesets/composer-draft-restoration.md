## zh-CN

恢复未发送的对话草稿时，输入框会等待草稿加载完成后再显示内容，不再出现占位文字覆盖正文或空回调清除草稿的问题。

## en

When restoring an unsent conversation draft, the composer now waits for the draft to load before showing its content, preventing placeholder text from covering the draft or an empty callback from clearing it.
