## zh-CN

在 Android 13 及以上版本，登录后进入首页时，如果还没有允许通知，Raft 会请求一次通知权限，新安装的用户不再错过通知。已经允许或之前询问过就不会再弹出；之后仍可在“设置 › 通知”中开启。

## en

On Android 13 and later, when you reach Home after signing in and notifications aren't allowed yet, Raft asks once for permission to send them, so new installs no longer miss notifications. It doesn't ask if they're already allowed or if it asked before; you can still turn them on in Settings › Notifications.
