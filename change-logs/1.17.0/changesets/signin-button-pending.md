## zh-CN

点击“登录到 Raft”后，按钮会显示“登录中…”并暂时不可点击，“登录到自托管服务器”也会暂时停用，避免重复发起登录。如果在浏览器里取消登录后回到应用，按钮会很快恢复，可以重新登录。

## en

After you tap Sign in to Raft, the button shows "Signing in…" and can't be tapped again, and Sign in to a self-hosted server is paused too, so a second sign-in doesn't start. If you cancel in the browser and come back, the button is ready again within a moment.
