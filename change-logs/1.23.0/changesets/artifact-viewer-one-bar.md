## zh-CN

在 App 里打开 Artifact 时，顶部只有一条栏了：之前 App 的标题栏下面还有网页自己的工具栏，两条栏占了不少地方。现在这一条栏里有返回、标题，标题下方是「#频道 · v3 ▾ · 5 人读过」，右侧是带评论数的「评论」按钮和 ⋮ 菜单。点版本号会列出所有版本（最新版有标记），选一个就切过去；点「评论」打开或收起评论列表。⋮ 里有复制链接、关注 / 取消关注、桌面模式（支持时）和在浏览器中打开。计数读取失败时只是不显示，页面照常打开。

## en

Opening an Artifact in the app now shows a single bar. Before, the web page's own toolbar appeared under the app's title bar, and the two took up a lot of room. The bar has Back and the title, with "#channel · v3 ▾ · 5 read" under it, plus a Comments button with the comment count and a ⋮ menu. Tap the version to list every version (the latest is marked) and switch to another one. Comments opens or closes the comment list. The ⋮ menu has Copy Link, Follow / Unfollow, Desktop Mode (where available) and Open in Browser. If the counts can't be loaded, they're left out and the page still opens.
