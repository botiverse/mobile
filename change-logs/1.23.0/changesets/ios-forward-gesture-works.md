## zh-CN

开启「对话前进手势（Beta）」后，从聊天返回首页，再从屏幕右边缘向左滑动，现在会重新打开刚才的聊天。首页列表加载完成后手势即可使用，不必再离开首页重进一次。设置里的说明也改得更清楚了。

## en

With "Conversation forward gesture (Beta)" on, going back from a chat to Home and then swiping left from the right edge of the screen now reopens that chat. The gesture works as soon as Home's list has loaded, without leaving Home and coming back first. The setting's description is clearer too.
