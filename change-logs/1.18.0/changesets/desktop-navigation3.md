## zh-CN

Compose 版：打开和关闭页面改用新的导航方式，为之后在大屏上把更多页面并排显示做准备。用起来和之前一样：Esc、Cmd+[、鼠标侧键返回和前进都照常可用，返回到的页面会停在离开时的位置。

## en

Compose desktop: opening and closing pages now runs on a new navigation model, groundwork for showing more pages side by side on wide windows. Nothing changes in use: Esc, Cmd+[ and the mouse back and forward buttons work as before, and a page you go back to is where you left it.

## ja
