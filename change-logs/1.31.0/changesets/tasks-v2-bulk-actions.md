## zh-CN

任务页新增批量选择与批量操作栏（与 Web 端对齐）：长按列表行或看板卡片进入多选模式，可全选或逐项勾选任务，通过底部悬浮操作栏批量修改状态、指派/清空负责人、关注任务会话或批量删除（带二次确认与失败项保留重试），并自动适配原生底栏覆盖与底部安全区。

## en

Added bulk selection and batch actions to the Tasks page (matching Web): long-press any list row or board card to enter selection mode, toggle individual tasks or use Select All, and apply bulk Status, Assignee (including Me and Unassigned), Follow, or Delete (with confirmation and retry of refused items) from the floating selection toolbar with native tab bar cover and bottom safe-area clearance.
