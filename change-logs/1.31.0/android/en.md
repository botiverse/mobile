✨ **New**
- **Tasks bulk actions & Activity Tasks filter**: Long-press any row or board card on **Tasks** to multi-select (or Select all) and batch-update status, assignee, follow, or delete (with retry for failed items); **Activity** adds a **Tasks** filter remembered per workspace.
- **In-app browser & web panel upgrades**: Added Back/Forward buttons, **Reload**, one-tap **Forward to Raft**, webpage dialogs, fullscreen video, and in-app new-window links while keeping **Open in browser** for Raft App pages; blocked automatic external app launches without a tap (adding confirmation prompts), added **Settings › Storage › In-app browser** to clear cache and cookies, and cleared isolated browser data on sign-out or account switch.
- **Expandable wide-screen composer & Markdown toolbar**: Wide screens add an **Expand / Collapse** composer button with a top Markdown formatting bar (extra actions fold into **More formatting**), Enter for newlines, and ⌘/Ctrl+Enter to send; switching chats, sending, or pressing Esc collapses while preserving drafts.
- **Usage-limit alerts & deleting Computers with others' agents**: Owners and admins get runtime usage-limit alerts in **Notifications** (affected Computers, agent counts, and reset times, synced across devices); deleting your Computer with others' agents still bound lists them so you can notify owners to move them or force unbind and delete.
- **Inline video download & quick reactions**: Inline chat video cards add a top-right **Download** button; added `🍻` as the 8th quick reaction after `✅`; pointer hover toolbars one-click toggle your most-used reaction (default 👍) and expand the full bar on brief hover.
- **Inline Artifact chips in bios & author subtitles**: Workspace Artifact links in author subtitles, profile previews, and member/agent bios now render as inline title chips (including deleted or unavailable states) opening straight into the viewer.
- **Persistent background connection for direct installs**: Direct-install builds add an opt-in **Persistent background connection** toggle (off by default) under **Settings › Notifications** for networks where Google push is unreachable.

🔧 **Improved**
- **Computers Needs attention summary, scans & diagnostics**: **Computers** adds a top **Needs attention** summary strip (cause, affected agents, recovery time, and quick filtering), clearer low-disk upgrade errors, uncached workspace scans ("at least…" partial counts), and version-specific reinstall steps for recently refused offline Computers; Agent "Report issue" on your own Computer offers a **Computer diagnostics bundle**, and **Feedback** accepts one diagnostics `.zip` (up to 10 MiB).
- **Clearer Agent setup, status & quota details**: Copy **Environment variables** from another agent on the same Computer (keeping existing keys, searchable above 8 entries), view wrapped Computer descriptions in Create/Move pickers, and pick directly between creating or connecting an external agent; usage-limit holds name the affected model, window, and reset time; agents needing action show unified orange indicators (including unavailable models with **Change model**, retired-runtime guidance, and fast-mode fallback notes).
- **External channels & redesigned external invites**: Redesigned **Invite people outside** with an email-first step, a **Connect your servers** footer (with public-channel rules), and a cleaner review screen; aligned external-channel controls across connected servers, let participant admins remove their own agents, kept members remaining via another server with a clear notice, and rendered historical external tasks read-only on participant servers.
- **Faster task sync, Artifact navigation & richer Mermaid diagrams**: Incremental sync applies task updates directly and loads **Tasks** counts in one request; Artifact links from tasks jump straight to the referenced message in the native task conversation (Markdown excerpts now wrap around top-right badges, and the transitional Artifact viewer browser button was removed); more Mermaid diagram types (block, Gantt, state, C4, mind map, architecture, Git, and Wardley) render inline.
- **Live Activity sync, smoother scrolling & faster resume**: Marking **Activity** items Done or undoing Done syncs live across devices; channel members and Agent Activity refresh quietly on resume or reconnect; improved smoothness when opening conversations, scrolling, and returning from the background.
- **Share icons, Settings search & visual polish**: System Share channel suggestions now show server icons with public, private, and external badges; indexed **Passkeys** and **Two-step verification** in Settings search; unified **Channel settings** naming and single-line menu sizing; removed extra external arrows on in-app Raft App links and lavender backdrops behind loaded app logos, head-truncated long domains in the web panel bar, synced **Show member descriptions** with sidebar DMs, added 7-day agent limit-hit counts to the 30-day history notice for admins, and switched light-mode warnings to amber.
- **Legacy preview update channel transition**: Retired the legacy `preview` update channel; existing `preview` installs automatically move to the main release channel on 1.31.0 and keep receiving in-app delta updates.

🐛 **Fixed**
- **Cleaner timelines & instant cached screens**: Removed mid-timeline loading and gap rows, limited boundary loading to one indicator at a time, cached quoted message link previews, stopped stale prewarmed snapshots when opening channels or DMs from **Activity**, and restored instant cached views with stuck-loading guards across **Computers**, Agent Detail, **Feedback**, and **Artifacts**.
- **Activity, tasks, replies & notification fixes**: Added Mute/Unmute and swipe Mute for App DMs in **Activity**, fixed Done items flashing back after live messages, fixed read **Activity** threads/tasks jumping to unreadable lines (reopening read tasks on wide screens), added Follow/Unfollow to reply-thread message menus, stopped duplicate notification pages and fixed **Saved**/**Drafts** phone shortcuts, excluded remote agents from Home's **Working now**, stopped agent-only notices counting as unread, preserved Codex access during bulk model edits, and fixed placeholder 1970 reset dates.
- **Inline chat media memory stability**: Automatically releases idle or off-screen chat video and audio players when scrolling away, switching pages, or backgrounding, preventing memory spikes, jank, and crashes.
- **In-app browser & Artifact reliability**: Fixed the Artifact viewer sticking on "Fetching page" after network errors (now showing **Retry** immediately), fixed false background timeouts or blank screens during sign-in or app switching, and prevented background Artifact prewarming from stalling foreground web pages.
- **Sessions, server switching & drafts**: Access refusals (403) now preserve your session and show the server's message instead of signing you out; creating a server lands directly on it; the server switcher scrolls the active server into view; locking the screen mid-switch recovers cleanly; stopped redundant startup checks for drafts on other servers or deleted channels.
- **Diagnostics logs, attachment picker & paused updates**: Fixed diagnostics flush failures dropping feedback archives, restored bounded recent logs in **Settings › Diagnostics / About** Copy & Share, fixed the attachment/camera picker getting stuck after unexpected dismissal, and fixed Update stalling after a Wi-Fi pre-download paused on mobile data.
- **Overlays, keyboard & visual fixes**: Fixed toasts appearing behind open dialogs/sheets, keyboard flashing when returning from a floating window, back navigation from a **Feedback** ticket skipping to **Settings**, link previews flashing at the bottom, and hover toolbar taps on touch+pointer devices; updated external-share headers to item counts, restored vibrant colors on all 8 reaction icons, tinted disabled-on switches yellow, and aligned header, avatar-ring, and **As task** spacing.
