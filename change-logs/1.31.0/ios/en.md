✨ **New**
- **Tasks bulk actions & Activity Tasks filter**: Long-press any row or board card on **Tasks** to multi-select (or Select all) and batch-update status, assignee, follow, or delete (with retry for failed items); **Activity** adds a **Tasks** filter remembered per workspace.
- **In-app browser & web panel upgrades**: Added Back/Forward buttons, **Reload**, one-tap **Forward to Raft**, and webpage dialogs while keeping **Open in browser** for Raft App pages; blocked automatic external app launches without a tap (adding confirmation prompts), added **Settings › Storage › In-app browser** to clear cache and cookies, and cleared isolated browser data on sign-out or account switch.
- **Expandable wide-screen composer & Markdown toolbar**: iPad and wide screens add an **Expand / Collapse** composer button with a top Markdown formatting bar (extra actions fold into **More formatting**), Enter for newlines, and ⌘/Ctrl+Enter to send; switching chats, sending, or pressing Esc collapses while preserving drafts.
- **Usage-limit alerts & deleting Computers with others' agents**: Owners and admins get runtime usage-limit alerts in **Notifications** (affected Computers, agent counts, and reset times, synced across devices); deleting your Computer with others' agents still bound lists them so you can notify owners to move them or force unbind and delete.
- **Inline video download & quick reactions**: Inline chat video cards add a top-right **Download** button; added `🍻` as the 8th quick reaction after `✅`; with a mouse or trackpad, hover toolbars one-click toggle your most-used reaction (default 👍) and expand the full bar on brief hover.
- **Inline Artifact chips in bios & author subtitles**: Workspace Artifact links in author subtitles, profile previews, and member/agent bios now render as inline title chips (including deleted or unavailable states) opening straight into the viewer.
- **Share-as-image width picker & TestFlight update checks**: Sharing messages as an image renders off-screen with a live preview and width presets (**Phone** 390, **Tablet** 600, **Wide** 768, or **Custom** 320–1080) so wide tables, code, and formulas wrap cleanly; TestFlight builds check for newer external TestFlight builds (including newer build numbers for the same version) in-app and under **Settings › About**, opening TestFlight directly.

🔧 **Improved**
- **Computers Needs attention summary, scans & diagnostics**: **Computers** adds a top **Needs attention** summary strip (cause, affected agents, recovery time, and quick filtering), clearer low-disk upgrade errors, uncached workspace scans ("at least…" partial counts), and version-specific reinstall steps for recently refused offline Computers; Agent "Report issue" on your own Computer offers a **Computer diagnostics bundle**, and **Feedback** accepts one diagnostics `.zip` (up to 10 MiB).
- **Clearer Agent setup, status & quota details**: Copy **Environment variables** from another agent on the same Computer (keeping existing keys, searchable above 8 entries), view wrapped Computer descriptions in Create/Move pickers, and pick directly between creating or connecting an external agent; usage-limit holds name the affected model, window, and reset time; agents needing action show unified orange indicators (including unavailable models with **Change model**, retired-runtime guidance, and fast-mode fallback notes).
- **External channels & redesigned external invites**: Redesigned **Invite people outside** with an email-first step, a **Connect your servers** footer (with public-channel rules), and a cleaner review screen; aligned external-channel controls across connected servers, let participant admins remove their own agents, kept members remaining via another server with a clear notice, and rendered historical external tasks read-only on participant servers.
- **Faster task sync, Artifact navigation & richer Mermaid diagrams**: Incremental sync applies task updates directly and loads **Tasks** counts in one request; Artifact links from tasks jump straight to the referenced message in the native task conversation (Markdown excerpts now wrap around top-right badges, and the transitional Artifact viewer browser button was removed); more Mermaid diagram types (block, Gantt, state, C4, mind map, architecture, Git, and Wardley) render inline.
- **Live Activity sync & smoother browsing**: Marking **Activity** items Done or undoing Done syncs live across devices; channel members and Agent Activity refresh quietly on resume or reconnect; improved smoothness when opening conversations, navigating back, and scrolling messages.
- **Share icons, Settings search & visual polish**: Share Sheet channel suggestions now show server icons with public, private, and external badges; indexed **Passkeys** and **Two-step verification** in Settings search; unified **Channel settings** naming and single-line menu sizing; removed extra external arrows on in-app Raft App links and lavender backdrops behind loaded app logos, head-truncated long domains in the web panel bar, synced **Show member descriptions** with sidebar DMs, added 7-day agent limit-hit counts to the 30-day history notice for admins, and switched light-mode warnings to amber.
- **iOS 26+ Liquid Glass server switcher & simpler navigation**: On iOS 26 and later, tapping the Home server switcher opens a native Liquid Glass popover in place with unread counts, drag reordering, and workspace actions; removed the experimental "Conversation forward gesture (Beta)" setting and right-edge forward swipe.

- **iPad cold launch, Share Sheet tab bar & diagnostics logs**: Fixed iPad cold launches showing the single-column phone layout until rotating or switching apps; fixed the floating tab bar covering the Continue button in the external-share picker (and updated its header to item counts); restored bounded recent logs in **Settings › Diagnostics / About** Copy & Share.
- **Swipe-back, popovers & visual fixes**: Fixed edge swipe-back from a **Feedback** ticket skipping to **Settings**, link previews flashing at the bottom, and hover toolbar taps on hybrid touch+pointer devices; restored vibrant colors on all 8 reaction icons, tinted disabled-on switches yellow, and aligned header, avatar-ring, and **As task** spacing.
