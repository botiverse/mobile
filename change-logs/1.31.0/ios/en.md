✨ **New**
- **Tasks bulk actions**: Long-press any task row or board card to multi-select and batch-update status, assignee, follow, or delete.
- **Tasks filter in Activity**: Filter **Activity** down to tasks, remembered per workspace.
- **In-app browser upgrades**: Installed Raft Apps now open in right-pushed pages with a floating tab pill switcher while ordinary links open in the system in-app browser; added Back/Forward, Reload, Forward to Raft, and cache/cookie clearing in **Settings › Storage**.
- **Expandable wide composer**: iPad and wide screens can expand the composer with a top Markdown formatting toolbar.
- **Direct video download**: Inline chat videos add a top-right Download button without opening preview.
- **Usage-limit & Computer delete alerts**: Admins get usage-limit alerts in **Notifications**; deleting a Computer with others' agents lets you notify owners to move them first.
- **Adjustable share-image width & TestFlight updates**: Pick the width when sharing messages as images; TestFlight builds can check for new builds in-app.

🔧 **Improved**
- **iOS 26+ Liquid Glass server switcher**: Opens an in-place popover from the home header, and removes the experimental right-edge forward gesture.
- **Computers status & diagnostics**: Added a **Needs attention** summary banner; bug reports can attach Computer diagnostics or a `.zip` diagnostic file.
- **Easier Agent management**: Copy environment variables from another Agent on the same Computer; clearer quota-pause and unavailable-model notices.
- **Streamlined external invites**: Simplified external-channel invites with a direct **Connect your server** path and unified cross-server permissions.
- **More Mermaid diagrams & live sync**: Added Gantt, state, mindmap, and more Mermaid charts; Activity **Done** state syncs live across devices.
- **Quick reactions & search**: Added `🍻` quick reaction and one-click pointer hover toggle; Settings search now finds **Passkeys** and **Two-factor authentication**.

🐛 **Fixed**
- Removed message-list loading gaps and flicker, showing cached screens immediately on return.
- Auto-released offscreen and background media players to stop memory spikes and jank in media-heavy chats.
- Fixed crashes when in-app web pages request microphone access, plus stuck Artifact loading and false browser timeouts.
- Fixed false sign-outs on permission errors, duplicate server-switch stacks, and AppSumo unlimited agent quota display.
- Fixed iPad cold launch opening in single-pane mode, share sheet tab bar covering Continue, and swipe-back overshooting Settings.
