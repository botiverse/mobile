## zh-CN

手机上主页列表的各个分组（置顶、频道、私信等）上下留白和网页版在手机上一致，空的分组也会留出相同的空间，列表不再显得挤在一起。

## en

On phones, the sections of the Home list (Pinned, Channels, Direct Messages and the rest) now have the same space above and below their rows as the web on a phone, including empty sections, so the list no longer looks cramped.
