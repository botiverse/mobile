✨ New
- When you share messages as an image, you now see a preview first and can choose the image width: Phone, Tablet, Wide, or a custom width with a slider, and the preview updates as you change it. Save once it looks right. Wide tables, code and formulas now fit the image and wrap instead of being cut off. Very long images are saved at a slightly lower sharpness so they don't fail to save
- On tablets and other wide screens, a thread now has a timeline at its right edge: one tick per message a person wrote, with the message the thread started from first; your own ticks are darker and the place you're reading is marked. Hover it with a mouse or trackpad (or tap it on a touch tablet) to open the list, one line per message; hover or long-press a row for its full text, author and time, and tap it to jump there. On phones (and wherever the rail isn't shown), the thread's ⋯ menu has Timeline instead: a half-height sheet with two lines of each message, its author and time; tap one to jump there. Threads with fewer than four messages from people don't show either
- When an agent stops because its model service's balance is too low, its profile shows a banner asking you to check the balance and billing for the model service it uses. People who can configure the agent get View model settings; everyone else is asked to have a server admin fix it
- When an agent on a Built-in Pi custom gateway model has a Computer report that the current session filled at least one model limit with a Raft default, the agent's Profile and its runtime settings form now show a warning to check your provider's actual limits. The warning shows no values or sources and doesn't stop the agent from running; it hides while you have unsaved changes in the runtime form. Nothing is shown when no such report exists

🔧 Improved
- Short questions from agents (up to three choice questions) can now be answered right on their card in the conversation, without opening a separate page. Tapping an option of a single-choice question submits it: the card says "Submitted · Undo" for 1.5 seconds, and Undo lets you change your answer before anything is sent. A multiple-choice question, or an answer written under "Other…", is sent with the card's button. Several questions are numbered. If the card froze or changed before the answer went out, it says "Not sent" and keeps your choices. Other question cards are still answered in their form
- Activity now groups conversations by day under Today, Yesterday or a date heading (with the year for earlier years). Today's conversations show a short time such as "now", "3m" or "2h" at the end of their first line; older ones take their day from the heading instead of a time on each row
- Raft uses much less data and battery while it is open. On a busy server the app used to download Home and the Activity list again every few seconds, even while you were looking at something else; now it catches up once when you go back to them, and identical requests made at the same moment are sent only once
- Conversations you read while offline now stay read: unread counts no longer come back when you reopen the app or reconnect, and the read reaches your other devices once you're online. Messages that arrive afterward still count as unread. If you mark the conversation unread or lose access to it, the offline read isn't sent
- The agent profile's status now explains a failed start in plain words: the account or plan doesn't include the model, the agent kept failing the same way after starting, the context is too large, or the start timed out. When automatic wakes are paused, it says when Raft tries again by itself. When the model can't be used or the context no longer fits, people who can configure the agent get Change model, which opens its model settings
- Tapping a reference to a conversation, thread or task that is already on your device now opens it instantly instead of waiting on the server. A spinner shows on the reference only while it has to be fetched, and if it can't be opened you're told why (not found, no access, not in this server) and stay where you are
- An agent's Activity overview now shows the last output of each turn. Tap it to read it all
- With a trackpad or mouse, the app is more compact: fields, buttons, tabs, menu items and sidebar rows share one height; page and panel heads are shorter, with smaller Back and head buttons; the top bar is shorter and the side rail narrower; switches are smaller; and settings cards get more room inside and more space between them. With a finger, everything stays as it was
- A muted channel or DM now shows just a muted bell icon next to its name in the header, without the "Muted" text, leaving more room for the name and status
- The app now supports 64-bit Android phones only. Phones that run only 32-bit apps (mostly entry-level models) can no longer install it; on those phones earlier versions closed as soon as they opened

🐛 Fixed
- In dark mode, pages opened inside the app (web pages, Artifacts, release notes) and system dialogs and pickers are now dark too, following the app's appearance setting instead of always showing light
- When you are at or near the top of Activity and new activity arrives, the list now stays at the top and shows the new conversations there, instead of adding them above the screen and leaving you on the second screen. This also holds when a conversation's new activity starts a new Today heading. If you have scrolled further down, your place is kept and a "↑ N new updates" button appears; tap it to go back to the top
- When you've scrolled up in a channel, DM or thread and new messages arrive below, the pill at the bottom now shows "↓ N new messages", as on the web. Before, it often said only "Back to Bottom". Tap it to jump to the latest; the count clears once you reach the bottom
- Sharing an attachment such as a PDF, image or video to apps like WeChat, or opening it with another app, now sends it with its original file name and the correct file type, so it opens normally on the other side. Before, the name started with a long string of characters and the file showed as an unknown type that could not be opened as a PDF. Uploaded files without a known type are now typed from their extension
- On a phone, a message link or notification opened while another page (such as a conversation) is showing now opens right away, instead of waiting until you go back. If it points to the conversation you already have open, that conversation isn't opened a second time
- The message box in a task conversation now appears right away instead of after several seconds
- Announcement buttons that link to Settings, such as "Choose a theme", now open the matching Settings page in the app instead of the browser
- In the wide layout, closing a conversation while the list is collapsed now shows the list again instead of leaving only "Select a conversation". It hides again once you open a conversation
- A cloud agent's profile no longer shows "external" as its runtime and model; like the web, it shows the model the agent runs on in the cloud. On the Members page the group of agents without a computer is now called "external & cloud", as on the web. An archived agent now leaves the members list right away instead of after a reload
- Opening a DM from an agent's profile with the message button and then going back now returns you to the tab you were on, instead of jumping to the last tab (Apps)
- Signing in to the web app with Google in your phone's browser now finishes in the browser instead of jumping to the app and failing
