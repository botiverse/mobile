## zh-CN

消息引用（如 `#频道 msg=…`、私信里的消息引用）现在和话题引用一样是青色；无法打开的应用提及显示为灰色。黄色只用于提及你自己。

## en

Message references (such as `#channel msg=…`, including ones in direct messages) are now cyan like thread references, and app mentions that can't be opened are gray. Yellow now only means a mention of you.

## ja
