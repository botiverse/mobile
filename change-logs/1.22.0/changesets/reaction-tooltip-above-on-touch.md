## zh-CN

长按表情回应查看谁回应了时，名单显示在表情上方，不会再被按住的手指挡住；上方放不下时才显示在下方。用鼠标悬停时仍显示在下方，和网页一致。

## en

Long-pressing a reaction now shows who reacted above the reaction, where your finger doesn't cover it (below only when there's no room above). Hovering with a mouse still shows it below, as on the web.
