## zh-CN

对话串中的音频附件（如 MP3、WAV、OGG、M4A、AAC、FLAC 等）现在支持内联播放：消息卡片内直接呈现专用音频控件，点击即可在原位播放与暂停，无需跳转外部应用，并继续提供文件名、文件大小与下载入口。

## en

Audio attachments in threads (including MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, and other audio formats) now support inline playback: a dedicated audio card renders directly inside the message, allowing playback and pause in place without navigating away, while continuing to provide file name, file size, and download options.
