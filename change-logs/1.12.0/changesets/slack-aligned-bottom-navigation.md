## zh-CN

新增 Slack 风格底部导航（受 `mobile_slack_nav_v0` 开关控制，默认关闭）：开启后底栏变为 Home（仅频道）/ DMs / Activity / More 四栏，DMs 页签展示微信风格的会话列表（头像、昵称、最新消息预览、相对时间与未读角标）。

## en

Added Slack-style bottom navigation behind the `mobile_slack_nav_v0` flag (off by default): when enabled, the tab bar becomes Home (channels only) / DMs / Activity / More, and the DMs tab shows a WeChat-style conversation list with avatars, names, latest message previews, relative timestamps, and unread badges.
