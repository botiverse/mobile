## zh-CN

开启 Slack 底栏模式时，新增独立的“私信”列表标签页：汇集所有单聊与群聊私信，以对话列表形式展示会话头像、名称、最新消息摘要与时间，点击即可直接进入对应对话串。

## en

When Slack bottom navigation mode is enabled, a dedicated "Direct Messages" tab is now available: aggregating all 1-on-1 and group direct messages into a unified conversation list showing avatars, names, latest message previews, and timestamps, with direct navigation to the corresponding thread upon tapping.
