## zh-CN

「我的消息对齐：右」模式下，对方的消息不再占满整行，改为与自己的消息一样限制最大宽度并在右侧留白。

## en

In the "My message alignment: right" layout, messages from others no longer fill the full row; they are capped to the same maximum width as your own messages and leave space on the right.
