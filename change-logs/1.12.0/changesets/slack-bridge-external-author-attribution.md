## zh-CN

Slack 桥接消息现在正常显示：外部发送者的消息不再被整条丢弃，显示其外部显示名、头像与平台标签（如 "Slack"）；点击头像会提示该用户来自外部平台而不是打开成员资料。

## en

Slack-bridged messages now render: external senders are no longer dropped, and their external display name, avatar, and provider label (e.g. "Slack") are shown; tapping the avatar hints that the user comes from an external platform instead of opening a member profile.
